术语表Glossary

正文中所有英文术语的中文对照与白话解释。手册正文保留英文原词——这是你在券商软件、财经媒体和实盘交流中实际会遇到的形态。 Chinese equivalents and plain-language explanations for every term used in the handbook. The main text keeps the English terms — the form you'll actually meet in broker platforms, financial media, and trading conversations.

Term 中文名Chinese 解释Explanation
Underlying标的 期权合约所指向的资产——股票、ETF 或指数。期权的价值完全派生于它，这也是"衍生品"一词的由来。 The asset a contract points at — a stock, ETF, or index. An option's value is derived entirely from it, which is where "derivative" comes from.
Contract Multiplier合约乘数 美股期权固定为 100：一张合约对应 100 股，报价也是"每股"价格。报价 3.20 意味着实付 $320。新手最贵的一个疏忽。 Fixed at 100 for US equity options: one contract covers 100 shares and quotes are per share. A quote of 3.20 costs $320. The most expensive beginner oversight there is.
Call看涨期权 在到期日前以 Strike 价格买入标的的权利。股价涨得越高，Call 越值钱。 The right to buy the underlying at the strike before expiration. The higher the stock goes, the more the call is worth.
Put看跌期权 在到期日前以 Strike 价格卖出标的的权利。股价跌得越深，Put 越值钱。 The right to sell the underlying at the strike before expiration. The lower the stock goes, the more the put is worth.
Strike行权价 合约约定的买卖价格。对卖方而言，它是你"预先承诺"的成交价——卖 Call 是预设卖价，卖 Put 是预设买价。 The contracted transaction price. For sellers, it's your pre-committed price — a short call sets your sale price, a short put your purchase price.
Premium权利金 买方为权利支付、卖方因义务收取的价格。由内在价值 + 时间价值构成。 What the buyer pays for the right and the seller collects for the obligation. Composed of intrinsic value plus time value.
Breakeven盈亏平衡点 到期时不赚不亏的股价。买方的 Breakeven 总是比 Strike 更远一个 Premium——这就是"方向对了还亏钱"的算术来源。 The price at expiry where you neither gain nor lose. A buyer's breakeven always sits one premium beyond the strike — the arithmetic behind "right on direction, still down money."
Expiration到期日 合约失效的日期。美股期权通常为周五。到期时 OTM 的期权价值归零。 The date the contract ceases to exist — usually a Friday for US equity options. OTM options expire worthless.
DTE剩余天数 Days to Expiration，距离到期的天数。"7 DTE"即一周后到期。Theta 衰减在低 DTE 时加速。 Days to expiration. "7 DTE" means one week left. Theta decay accelerates at low DTE.
ITM / ATM / OTM价内 / 平值 / 价外 In / At / Out of the Money。以 Call 为例：股价高于 Strike 为 ITM（行权有利可图），约等于为 ATM，低于为 OTM。Put 方向相反。 In / at / out of the money. For a call: stock above strike is ITM (exercising is profitable), near it ATM, below it OTM. Reversed for puts.
Intrinsic Value内在价值 立刻行权能拿到的价值。Call 为 max(股价 − Strike, 0)。OTM 期权的内在价值为零，价格全部是时间价值。 What exercising right now would be worth: for a call, max(stock − strike, 0). OTM options have zero intrinsic value — their price is all time value.
Time Value / Extrinsic时间价值 Premium 中超出内在价值的部分，是市场为"到期前还可能发生的事"支付的价格。随时间流逝归零。 The part of the premium beyond intrinsic value — the market's price for "what might still happen before expiry." Decays to zero over time.
Implied Volatility (IV)隐含波动率 由期权市价反推出的、市场对未来波动幅度的预期。IV 越高，期权越贵。它衡量的是"预期的动荡程度"，不是方向。 The future volatility implied by current option prices — the market's expectation of turbulence, not direction. Higher IV, pricier options.
IV Crush隐波塌缩 重大事件（如财报）落地后 IV 骤降，期权时间价值被瞬间抽走。财报前买期权、财报后"方向对了还亏钱"的元凶。 The sharp IV collapse after a major event (like earnings) resolves, vacuuming out time value. The classic reason a directionally correct earnings bet still loses money.
Delta德尔塔 股价每变动 $1，期权价格变动多少。也常被当作"到期时 ITM 的粗略概率"——0.30 Delta 的 Put 约有三成概率被指派。 How much the option moves per $1 move in the stock. Also a rough proxy for the probability of expiring ITM — a 0.30-delta put has ~30% assignment odds.
Gamma伽马 Delta 的变化速度。临近到期的 ATM 期权 Gamma 最大——价格对股价变得极其敏感，这也是 Gamma Squeeze 的燃料。 The rate of change of delta. Near-expiry ATM options have the highest gamma — prices become hypersensitive to the stock. This is the fuel of a gamma squeeze.
Theta西塔 / 时间衰减 每天流逝的时间价值。买方的 Theta 为负（每天被扣钱），卖方为正（每天收租）。临近到期加速。 Time value lost per day. Negative for buyers (a daily toll), positive for sellers (daily rent). Accelerates near expiry.
Vega维加 IV 每变动 1 个百分点，期权价格变动多少。财报前买期权就是在做多 Vega——而 IV Crush 正是 Vega 的反噬。 Option price change per one-point move in IV. Buying pre-earnings options is a long-vega bet — and IV crush is vega's revenge.
Assignment行权指派 卖方被要求履行义务：Short Put 被指派要按 Strike 买入 100 股，Short Call 被指派要按 Strike 交出 100 股。 The seller being called to perform: an assigned short put must buy 100 shares at the strike; an assigned short call must deliver 100 shares at the strike.
Exercise行权 买方主动使用权利。美式期权（多数个股期权）到期前任何时候都可行权；实践中多数人选择直接卖出合约而非行权。 The buyer invoking the right. American-style options (most equity options) can be exercised any time before expiry; in practice most holders sell the contract instead.
American / European美式 / 欧式 行权方式。美式可在到期日之前任何时候行权（美股个股期权基本都是），欧式只能在到期日当天（多数指数期权，如 SPX）。对买方通常无关紧要，对卖方决定了"最坏的那天别人能不能敲门"。 Exercise style. American options can be exercised any time before expiry (essentially all US single-stock options); European ones only on the expiration date (most index options, e.g. SPX). Rarely matters to buyers; for sellers it decides whether anyone can knock on the worst day.
Covered Call备兑看涨 持有 100 股 + 卖出 1 张 Call。用 Strike 之上的上涨空间换取确定的 Premium。见第 2.4 节。 100 shares plus one short call. Trades upside beyond the strike for certain premium. See §2.4.
Cash-Secured Put (CSP)现金担保卖出看跌 卖出 1 张 Put 并锁定足额现金准备接货。本质是"别人付钱请你挂的限价买单"。见第 2.5 节。 One short put backed by full cash to buy if assigned. Essentially a limit buy order someone pays you to place. See §2.5.
Protective Put保护性买入看跌 持股 + 买入 Put，给持仓上保险：Premium 是保费，Strike 是理赔起付线。见第 2.3 节。 Shares plus a long put — insurance on the position: the premium is the cost, the strike is the payout line. See §2.3.
Spread价差组合 同时买卖不同 Strike 或到期日的期权，把风险和收益都框定在一个区间内。第 4 章的主角。 Simultaneously buying and selling options at different strikes or expiries, boxing both risk and reward into a range. The subject of Chapter 4.
Vertical Spread垂直价差 同标的、同到期日、同类型、方向相反、Strike 不同的两条腿。"垂直"来自期权链上同一到期日的合约纵向排列。第 4 章的主题。 Two legs on the same underlying, same expiry, same type, opposite directions, different strikes. "Vertical" because one expiry's contracts run down an option chain. The subject of Chapter 4.
Debit / Credit Spread借记 / 贷记价差 开仓时净付出权利金的是 Debit（借记，如 Bull Call Spread、Bear Put Spread）；净收到权利金的是 Credit（贷记，如 Bull Put Spread、Bear Call Spread）。前者 Theta 为负，后者为正。 A debit spread pays premium to open (bull call, bear put); a credit spread receives it (bull put, bear call). Debit spreads carry negative theta, credit spreads positive.
Width价差宽度 两条腿 Strike 之间的差额。它决定了结构的总空间：借记价差最大盈利 = 宽度 − 净借记；贷记价差最大亏损 = 宽度 − 净贷记。 The gap between the two strikes — the structure's total space. Debit spread max gain = width − net debit; credit spread max loss = width − net credit.
Defined Risk定义风险 最坏结果在开仓那一刻就已确定且有限的结构。价差、Iron Condor 都属此类；Naked Short 不属于。定义风险不等于低风险——它只保证你知道上限是多少。 A structure whose worst case is known and finite at entry. Spreads and iron condors qualify; a naked short does not. Defined risk is not low risk — it only guarantees you know the ceiling.
Straddle / Strangle跨式 / 宽跨式 同时买入（或卖出）Call 和 Put，做多（或做空）波动本身而不押方向。Strike 相同为 Straddle，分开为 Strangle。 Buying (or selling) both a call and a put — a bet on volatility itself, not direction. Same strike: straddle; separated strikes: strangle.
Iron Condor铁鹰 同时卖出上下两个价外价差，赌股价停留在区间内。收租策略的进阶形态，风险有限但两侧都可能破。 Selling an OTM spread on each side, betting the stock stays in a range. An advanced income structure — defined risk, but breachable on either wing.
Collar领口 持股 + 买 Put + 卖 Call：用卖 Call 的收入补贴买 Put 的保费，把持仓收益锁进一个区间，常可做到零成本。 Shares + long put + short call: the call premium subsidizes the put, boxing the position into a range — often at zero net cost.
Roll移仓 / 展期 平掉当前合约、同时开出更远到期日（或不同 Strike）的新合约。卖方管理头寸、推迟指派的核心操作。 Closing the current contract while opening a later-dated (or different-strike) one. The seller's core maneuver for managing positions and deferring assignment.
The Wheel车轮策略 CSP 收租 → 被指派接货 → 转卖 Covered Call → 被 call 走 → 回到 CSP 的循环系统。第 7 章专讲。 The cycle: CSP for rent → assignment → covered calls → called away → back to CSP. Chapter 7's subject.
Naked Short裸卖 无股票、无现金抵押地卖出期权。裸卖 Call 的理论亏损无限。本手册所有卖方策略都要求有抵押。 Selling options with no shares or cash behind them. A naked call has theoretically unlimited loss. Every short strategy in this handbook is collateralized.
Margin保证金 券商要求为潜在义务预留的资金。保证金不足会触发 Margin Call，极端时券商会强制平仓——不与你商量。 Capital the broker requires against potential obligations. A shortfall triggers a margin call; in extremes the broker liquidates for you — without asking.
Gamma Squeeze伽马挤压 大量买入 Call → 做市商买股对冲 → 股价上涨 → Delta 上升需买入更多 → 正反馈螺旋。2021 年 GameStop 的引擎。 Heavy call buying → market makers hedge by buying stock → stock rises → deltas rise, forcing more buying → a feedback spiral. The engine of GameStop 2021.
Regime市场状态 当前市场所处的宏观环境类型（单边牛、震荡、趋势熊、恐慌）。同一策略在不同 Regime 下结果天差地别——先判断状态，再选策略。 The prevailing market environment (one-way bull, chop, trending bear, panic). The same strategy produces wildly different outcomes across regimes — read the regime before choosing the strategy.
