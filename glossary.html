术语表Glossary

正文中所有英文术语的中文对照与白话解释。手册正文保留英文原词——这是你在券商软件、财经媒体和实盘交流中实际会遇到的形态。 Chinese equivalents and plain-language explanations for every term used in the handbook. The main text keeps the English terms — the form you'll actually meet in broker platforms, financial media, and trading conversations.

Term 中文名Chinese 解释Explanation
Underlying标的 期权合约所指向的资产——股票、ETF 或指数。期权的价值完全派生于它，这也是"衍生品"一词的由来。 The asset a contract points at — a stock, ETF, or index. An option's value is derived entirely from it, which is where "derivative" comes from.
Contract Multiplier合约乘数 美股期权固定为 100：一张合约对应 100 股，报价也是"每股"价格。报价 3.20 意味着实付 $320。新手最贵的一个疏忽。 Fixed at 100 for US equity options: one contract covers 100 shares and quotes are per share. A quote of 3.20 costs $320. The most expensive beginner oversight there is.
Call看涨期权 在到期日前以 Strike 价格买入标的的权利。股价涨得越高，Call 越值钱。 The right to buy the underlying at the strike before expiration. The higher the stock goes, the more the call is worth.
Put看跌期权 在到期日前以 Strike 价格卖出标的的权利。股价跌得越深，Put 越值钱。 The right to sell the underlying at the strike before expiration. The lower the stock goes, the more the put is worth.
Strike行权价 合约约定的买卖价格。对卖方而言，它是你"预先承诺"的成交价——卖 Call 是预设卖价，卖 Put 是预设买价。 The contracted transaction price. For sellers, it's your pre-committed price — a short call sets your sale price, a short put your purchase price.
Premium权利金 买方为权利支付、卖方因义务收取的价格。由内在价值 + 时间价值构成。 What the buyer pays for the right and the seller collects for the obligation. Composed of intrinsic value plus time value.
Breakeven盈亏平衡点 到期时不赚不亏的股价。买方的 Breakeven 总是比 Strike 更远一个 Premium——这就是"方向对了还亏钱"的算术来源。 The price at expiry where you neither gain nor lose. A buyer's breakeven always sits one premium beyond the strike — the arithmetic behind "right on direction, still down money."
Expiration到期日 合约失效的日期。美股期权通常为周五。到期时 OTM 的期权价值归零。 The date the contract ceases to exist — usually a Friday for US equity options. OTM options expire worthless.
DTE剩余天数 Days to Expiration，距离到期的天数。"7 DTE"即一周后到期。Theta 衰减在低 DTE 时加速。 Days to expiration. "7 DTE" means one week left. Theta decay accelerates at low DTE.
ITM / ATM / OTM价内 / 平值 / 价外 In / At / Out of the Money。以 Call 为例：股价高于 Strike 为 ITM（行权有利可图），约等于为 ATM，低于为 OTM。Put 方向相反。 In / at / out of the money. For a call: stock above strike is ITM (exercising is profitable), near it ATM, below it OTM. Reversed for puts.
Intrinsic Value内在价值 立刻行权能拿到的价值。Call 为 max(股价 − Strike, 0)。OTM 期权的内在价值为零，价格全部是时间价值。 What exercising right now would be worth: for a call, max(stock − strike, 0). OTM options have zero intrinsic value — their price is all time value.
Time Value / Extrinsic时间价值 Premium 中超出内在价值的部分，是市场为"到期前还可能发生的事"支付的价格。随时间流逝归零。 The part of the premium beyond intrinsic value — the market's price for "what might still happen before expiry." Decays to zero over time.
Implied Volatility (IV)隐含波动率 由期权市价反推出的、市场对未来波动幅度的预期。IV 越高，期权越贵。它衡量的是"预期的动荡程度"，不是方向。 The future volatility implied by current option prices — the market's expectation of turbulence, not direction. Higher IV, pricier options.
IV Crush隐波塌缩 重大事件（如财报）落地后 IV 骤降，期权时间价值被瞬间抽走。财报前买期权、财报后"方向对了还亏钱"的元凶。 The sharp IV collapse after a major event (like earnings) resolves, vacuuming out time value. The classic reason a directionally correct earnings bet still loses money.
Delta德尔塔 股价每变动 $1，期权价格变动多少。也常被当作"到期时 ITM 的粗略概率"——0.30 Delta 的 Put 约有三成概率被指派。 How much the option moves per $1 move in the stock. Also a rough proxy for the probability of expiring ITM — a 0.30-delta put has ~30% assignment odds.
Gamma伽马 Delta 的变化速度。临近到期的 ATM 期权 Gamma 最大——价格对股价变得极其敏感，这也是 Gamma Squeeze 的燃料。 The rate of change of delta. Near-expiry ATM options have the highest gamma — prices become hypersensitive to the stock. This is the fuel of a gamma squeeze.
Theta西塔 / 时间衰减 每天流逝的时间价值。买方的 Theta 为负（每天被扣钱），卖方为正（每天收租）。临近到期加速。 Time value lost per day. Negative for buyers (a daily toll), positive for sellers (daily rent). Accelerates near expiry.
Vega维加 IV 每变动 1 个百分点，期权价格变动多少。财报前买期权就是在做多 Vega——而 IV Crush 正是 Vega 的反噬。 Option price change per one-point move in IV. Buying pre-earnings options is a long-vega bet — and IV crush is vega's revenge.
Volatility Skew波动率偏斜 同一到期日、不同 Strike 的 IV 并不相同。股指期权的价外 Put 的 IV 通常显著高于价外 Call——市场为下跌保险付的钱更多。1987 年黑色星期一之后才形成的现象。 IV differs across strikes at the same expiry. In index options, OTM puts carry markedly higher IV than OTM calls — the market pays more for downside insurance. The pattern only emerged after Black Monday 1987.
Delta HedgingDelta 对冲 通过买卖正股把头寸的 Delta 抵消至零，从而只保留波动率暴露。做市商的日常工作；也是 Gamma Squeeze 中被迫买入的那股力量。 Buying or selling the underlying to cancel a position's delta, leaving only volatility exposure. A market maker's daily job — and the forced buying behind a gamma squeeze.
VIX波动率指数 由标普 500 期权价格反推的未来 30 天预期波动率，俗称"恐慌指数"。把它读成保单价格指数更准确：长期均值约 19，2020 年 3 月曾收在 82.69。 Expected 30-day S&P 500 volatility implied by option prices — the "fear index." More accurately read as an insurance price index: long-run average near 19, with a 82.69 close in March 2020.
Realized Volatility实际波动率 标的已经走出的波动幅度（历史数据算出）。与 IV 的差额是期权卖方的真实利润来源：卖方赚钱的条件是 IV > 实际波动率，而不是"股价不动"。 How much the underlying actually moved, computed from history. The gap against IV is an option seller's true source of profit: sellers win when IV exceeds realized volatility, not when the stock stands still.
IV Rank / PercentileIV 分位 当前 IV 在过去一年区间中的相对位置（0–100）。回答"现在的期权是贵还是便宜"——绝对的 IV 数字在不同标的间不可比，分位数可以。 Where current IV sits within its own trailing-year range (0–100). It answers "are options expensive right now" — absolute IV is not comparable across underlyings, but the rank is.
Assignment行权指派 卖方被要求履行义务：Short Put 被指派要按 Strike 买入 100 股，Short Call 被指派要按 Strike 交出 100 股。 The seller being called to perform: an assigned short put must buy 100 shares at the strike; an assigned short call must deliver 100 shares at the strike.
Exercise行权 买方主动使用权利。美式期权（多数个股期权）到期前任何时候都可行权；实践中多数人选择直接卖出合约而非行权。 The buyer invoking the right. American-style options (most equity options) can be exercised any time before expiry; in practice most holders sell the contract instead.
American / European美式 / 欧式 行权方式。美式可在到期日之前任何时候行权（美股个股期权基本都是），欧式只能在到期日当天（多数指数期权，如 SPX）。对买方通常无关紧要，对卖方决定了"最坏的那天别人能不能敲门"。 Exercise style. American options can be exercised any time before expiry (essentially all US single-stock options); European ones only on the expiration date (most index options, e.g. SPX). Rarely matters to buyers; for sellers it decides whether anyone can knock on the worst day.
Covered Call备兑看涨 持有 100 股 + 卖出 1 张 Call。用 Strike 之上的上涨空间换取确定的 Premium。见第 2.4 节。 100 shares plus one short call. Trades upside beyond the strike for certain premium. See §2.4.
Cash-Secured Put (CSP)现金担保卖出看跌 卖出 1 张 Put 并锁定足额现金准备接货。本质是"别人付钱请你挂的限价买单"。见第 2.5 节。 One short put backed by full cash to buy if assigned. Essentially a limit buy order someone pays you to place. See §2.5.
Protective Put保护性买入看跌 持股 + 买入 Put，给持仓上保险：Premium 是保费，Strike 是理赔起付线。见第 2.3 节。 Shares plus a long put — insurance on the position: the premium is the cost, the strike is the payout line. See §2.3.
Spread价差组合 同时买卖不同 Strike 或到期日的期权，把风险和收益都框定在一个区间内。第 4 章的主角。 Simultaneously buying and selling options at different strikes or expiries, boxing both risk and reward into a range. The subject of Chapter 4.
Put-Call Parity平价关系 C − P = S − K·e−rT。套利恒等式，不是模型——右边没有波动率，不依赖任何关于股价行为的假设。它推出"同 Strike 的 Call 与 Put 必然同 IV"，也解释了为什么不同 Strike 之间可以有偏斜。严格形式要求欧式行权。见第 10 章。 C − P = S − K·e−rT. An arbitrage identity, not a model — no volatility on the right-hand side and no assumption about how the stock behaves. It implies a call and put at one strike must share an IV, and explains why skew across strikes is permitted. The strict form requires European exercise. See Chapter 10.
Synthetic Position合成头寸 用期权腿复制另一个头寸的盈亏形状。由平价关系可得：+C −P = 持股，持股 +P = 合成 Call，而 Covered Call ≡ Short Put（差一个等于现金利息的常数）。选哪种表达是成本问题，不是风险问题。 Replicating one position's payoff with option legs. From parity: +C −P is long stock, stock +P is a synthetic call, and a covered call ≡ a short put (differing by a constant equal to the interest on the cash). Which expression to use is a cost question, not a risk one.
Box Spread箱体价差 在两个 Strike 上分别建合成多头与合成空头。到期价值恒等于宽度、与股价无关——它是一张零息债券：买入即放贷，卖出即借款。用美式期权做会因提前行权而失效，2019 年 1R0NYMAN 即因此爆仓。 A synthetic long at one strike and a synthetic short at another. Its expiry value equals the width regardless of the stock — it is a zero-coupon bond: buying lends, selling borrows. Built with American options it breaks via early exercise, which is how 1R0NYMAN blew up in 2019.
Early Exercise提前行权 美式期权持有人在到期前主动行权。对买方通常不划算，但深度价内或除息日前是例外——而它一旦发生，任何依赖"所有腿同时到期"的结构都会被拆开一条腿。与指派是同一件事的两端。 An American option's holder exercising before expiry. Usually suboptimal for the buyer, with deep-ITM positions and ex-dividend dates as the exceptions — and when it happens, any structure relying on "all legs settle together" loses a leg. It is the other end of assignment.
Term Structure期限结构 同一 Strike 下 IV 随到期日变化的曲线。平静时向上倾斜（contango），恐慌时整体倒挂。自 2006 年以来 VIX 曲线仅约 19% 的交易日处于倒挂，主要集中在 2008–09、2020-03 等危机时段。见第 11.3 节。 How IV varies across expiries at one strike. It slopes up in calm markets (contango) and inverts wholesale in panics. Since 2006 the VIX curve has been inverted on only about 19% of trading days, mostly in crisis stretches such as 2008–09 and March 2020. See §11.3.
Diagonal Spread对角价差 同时改变 Strike 与到期日的两腿结构——垂直价差与日历价差的合体。最流行的形态是 PMCC。 A two-leg structure varying both strike and expiry — a vertical and a calendar combined. Its most popular form is the PMCC.
PMCC穷人的备兑看涨 Poor Man's Covered Call：用深度价内的长期 Call（LEAPS）替代 100 股正股，再卖出价外月度 Call 收租。资金占用可降约 74%，但那不是效率，是杠杆——2022 年 4 月 NFLX 一夜跌 35.1%，同资金口径下 PMCC 的亏损是传统 Covered Call 的 1.86 倍。见第 11.4 节。 Poor man's covered call: substitute a deep-ITM long-dated call (LEAPS) for 100 shares, then sell monthly OTM calls against it. Capital can fall about 74% — but that is leverage, not efficiency: when NFLX fell 35.1% overnight in April 2022, on equal capital the PMCC lost 1.86× what a classic covered call did. See §11.4.
LEAPS长期期权 到期日在一年以上的期权。Vega 大、Gamma 小、每日 Theta 损耗慢，因此常被用作正股替身——但这个替身只在保持深度价内时才成立。 Options expiring more than a year out. High vega, low gamma and slow daily theta make them a common stock substitute — a substitution that only holds while they stay deep ITM.
Vertical Spread垂直价差 同标的、同到期日、同类型、方向相反、Strike 不同的两条腿。"垂直"来自期权链上同一到期日的合约纵向排列。第 4 章的主题。 Two legs on the same underlying, same expiry, same type, opposite directions, different strikes. "Vertical" because one expiry's contracts run down an option chain. The subject of Chapter 4.
Debit / Credit Spread借记 / 贷记价差 开仓时净付出权利金的是 Debit（借记，如 Bull Call Spread、Bear Put Spread）；净收到权利金的是 Credit（贷记，如 Bull Put Spread、Bear Call Spread）。前者 Theta 为负，后者为正。 A debit spread pays premium to open (bull call, bear put); a credit spread receives it (bull put, bear call). Debit spreads carry negative theta, credit spreads positive.
Width价差宽度 两条腿 Strike 之间的差额。它决定了结构的总空间：借记价差最大盈利 = 宽度 − 净借记；贷记价差最大亏损 = 宽度 − 净贷记。 The gap between the two strikes — the structure's total space. Debit spread max gain = width − net debit; credit spread max loss = width − net credit.
Defined Risk定义风险 最坏结果在开仓那一刻就已确定且有限的结构。价差、Iron Condor 都属此类；Naked Short 不属于。定义风险不等于低风险——它只保证你知道上限是多少。 A structure whose worst case is known and finite at entry. Spreads and iron condors qualify; a naked short does not. Defined risk is not low risk — it only guarantees you know the ceiling.
Straddle / Strangle跨式 / 宽跨式 同时买入（或卖出）Call 和 Put，做多（或做空）波动本身而不押方向。Strike 相同为 Straddle，分开为 Strangle。 Buying (or selling) both a call and a put — a bet on volatility itself, not direction. Same strike: straddle; separated strikes: strangle.
Expected Move预期波动幅度 期权市场对"到期前标的会走多远"的报价。快速估法：ATM Straddle 的总价 ÷ 股价。买波动率之前必须先算它——你赌的不是"会动"，而是"动得比这个数更大"。 The option market's quote for how far the underlying travels before expiry. Quick estimate: the ATM straddle's total price ÷ the share price. Compute it before buying volatility — your bet isn't "it moves," it's "it moves more than this."
Short Volatility做空波动率 任何净卖出期权的头寸（Short Strangle、Iron Condor、CSP、Covered Call）。收益分布天然是"缓坡 + 悬崖"：多数时候小赚，偶尔一次大亏。见第 5.5 节与第 8 章。 Any net-short-option position (short strangle, iron condor, CSP, covered call). Its return distribution is inherently "slope then cliff": small gains most of the time, an occasional large loss. See §5.5 and Chapter 8.
Butterfly蝶式 买 1 张低 Strike、卖 2 张中 Strike、买 1 张高 Strike，行权价等距。第一个能表达"到期落在这一带"的结构。它赌的是两个平衡点之间的区间，不是一个点——最大亏损恒为净借记，无论股价跑到哪里。见第 12 章。 Long one low strike, short two at the middle, long one high, equally spaced. The first structure able to say "it lands in this band." It bets on the range between its breakevens, not on a point — and its maximum loss is the net debit however far the stock travels. See Chapter 12.
Broken-Wing Butterfly断翼蝶 把蝶式的一只翅膀往外挪，两侧宽度不等，成本下降甚至转为净收权利金。常被宣传为"一侧无风险"——近侧确实封死，但挪远留下的宽度差成为新的最大亏损，通常是权利金的数倍。它没有消除风险，只是把风险搬到了一侧。 A butterfly with one wing pushed further out, so the sides are unequal, the cost drops and it may even open for a credit. Marketed as "no risk on one side" — the near side is genuinely sealed, but the widened gap becomes the new maximum loss, typically several times the credit. It relocates risk rather than removing it.
Ratio Spread比率价差 买 1 卖 2（或更不平衡）。第二条空头腿没有任何东西覆盖，那个方向没有上限——图形有帐篷、名字很专业，但本质是第 8 章的裸卖换了外表。2022-11-10 SPY 一天涨 5.6%，一个开仓净收 $0.87 的 1×2 单日亏 $476。见第 12.4 节。 Buy one, sell two (or worse). The second short leg has nothing covering it, so that direction has no ceiling — a tidy tent and a professional name, but structurally Chapter 8's naked short in better clothes. When SPY rose 5.6% on 2022-11-10, a 1×2 opened for a $0.87 credit lost $476 in a session. See §12.4.
Backspread反向比率 比率价差的镜像：卖 1 买 2。净多头腿数为正，所以尾部向外敞开——小幅波动小亏，剧烈单边行情收益无上限。本书唯一一个希望自己错得离谱的结构。 The mirror of a ratio: sell one, buy two. Net long leg count is positive, so the tail opens outward — small losses on modest moves, unbounded gains on a violent one. The only structure in this book that hopes to be spectacularly wrong.
Pin Risk钉住风险 到期时标的恰好贴在你的空头行权价上，你在下周一开盘前无法知道自己是否被指派；部分指派会留下一个意料之外的正股头寸，而对冲它的腿已经作废。研究显示到期日收盘价确实向行权价聚集（约 8.2% vs 相邻日不超过 6%），机制是做市商 Delta 对冲。实务处理：不要把多腿结构留到收盘。 At expiry the underlying sits right on your short strike and you cannot know whether you were assigned until Monday's open; a partial assignment leaves an unexpected stock position whose hedging legs have already expired. Closing prices really do cluster at strikes on expiration days (about 8.2% versus never above 6% on adjacent days), driven by dealer delta hedging. Practical handling: don't carry a multi-leg structure into the closing bell.
Iron Condor铁鹰 同时卖出上下两个价外价差，赌股价停留在区间内。收租策略的进阶形态，风险有限但两侧都可能破。 Selling an OTM spread on each side, betting the stock stays in a range. An advanced income structure — defined risk, but breachable on either wing.
Calendar Spread日历价差 卖出近月、买入远月的同 Strike 合约。近月的事件溢价更高、塌得更狠，所以你卖掉塌得最狠的那个。为"确信 IV 会塌但不想赌方向"设计。见第 6.5 节。 Sell the near-dated contract and buy the far-dated one at the same strike. The near leg holds more event premium and crushes harder, so you sell the one that collapses most. Built for "I'm sure IV falls but I don't want a directional bet." See §6.5.
Collar领口 持股 + 买 Put + 卖 Call：用卖 Call 的收入补贴买 Put 的保费，把持仓收益锁进一个区间，常可做到零成本。 Shares + long put + short call: the call premium subsidizes the put, boxing the position into a range — often at zero net cost.
Cost Basis成本基准 扣除已收权利金后的实际持股成本：被指派时 = Strike − 累计权利金。它是 Wheel 里所有 Covered Call 的 Strike 下限——把 Strike 定在它之下，被 call 走就锁定亏损。见第 7.3 节。 Your real cost after netting off premiums collected: on assignment, strike − cumulative premium. It is the floor for every covered-call strike in a Wheel — set a strike below it and being called away locks in a loss. See §7.3.
Roll移仓 / 展期 平掉当前合约、同时开出更远到期日（或不同 Strike）的新合约。卖方管理头寸、推迟指派的核心操作。 Closing the current contract while opening a later-dated (or different-strike) one. The seller's core maneuver for managing positions and deferring assignment.
The Wheel车轮策略 CSP 收租 → 被指派接货 → 转卖 Covered Call → 被 call 走 → 回到 CSP 的循环系统。第 7 章专讲。 The cycle: CSP for rent → assignment → covered calls → called away → back to CSP. Chapter 7's subject.
Naked Short裸卖 无股票、无现金抵押地卖出期权。裸卖 Call 的理论亏损无限。本手册所有卖方策略都要求有抵押。 Selling options with no shares or cash behind them. A naked call has theoretically unlimited loss. Every short strategy in this handbook is collateralized.
ETN交易所交易票据 Exchange-Traded Note，由发行方承诺兑付某个指数收益的无担保债券——不是基金，你持有的是对发行方的债权，且条款里可能藏着提前终止权。XIV 就是这样在 2018 年被清算的。 An exchange-traded note: an unsecured debt obligation in which the issuer promises an index's return. Not a fund — you hold a claim on the issuer, and the terms may contain an early-termination right. That is how XIV was liquidated in 2018.
Contango / Backwardation期货升水 / 贴水 远月期货价格高于近月为 Contango，反之为 Backwardation。VIX 期货长期处于 Contango，这正是做空波动率能长年获利的结构性来源——也是它看起来像免费午餐的原因。 Contango is later-dated futures priced above nearer ones; backwardation is the reverse. VIX futures sit in persistent contango, which is the structural source of short-volatility profits over the years — and why it can look like a free lunch.
Tail Risk尾部风险 概率极低但后果极大的事件。它的危险不在于难以预测，而在于它出现的频率低到足以让人在它出现前把仓位加满。见第 8 章的三个案例。 Events of very low probability and very large consequence. Their danger isn't unpredictability so much as that they occur rarely enough for people to max out their size before one arrives. See Chapter 8's three cases.
Portfolio Insurance组合保险 1980 年代流行的策略：不买 Put，而是靠"下跌时动态卖出股指期货"来复制 Put 的收益。它依赖"随时能按模型价格成交"，而这个前提在 1987-10-19 消失了。合约与复制策略的区别，只在你需要它的那天显现。 A 1980s strategy: instead of buying puts, replicate their payoff by dynamically selling index futures as the market falls. It assumes you can always transact near model prices — an assumption that vanished on 1987-10-19. The difference between a contract and a replication only shows up on the day you need it.
Margin保证金 券商要求为潜在义务预留的资金。保证金不足会触发 Margin Call，极端时券商会强制平仓——不与你商量。 Capital the broker requires against potential obligations. A shortfall triggers a margin call; in extremes the broker liquidates for you — without asking.
Gamma Squeeze伽马挤压 大量买入 Call → 做市商买股对冲 → 股价上涨 → Delta 上升需买入更多 → 正反馈螺旋。2021 年 GameStop 的引擎。 Heavy call buying → market makers hedge by buying stock → stock rises → deltas rise, forcing more buying → a feedback spiral. The engine of GameStop 2021.
Kelly Criterion凯利公式 使长期复合增长率最大化的下注比例。它是一座山不是一条上升线：下注到两倍最优值，即使优势真实，长期增长率也变成负数。实践中通常只用一半（半凯利），保留约 75% 的增长并为"高估优势"留出余地。见第 9.3 节。 The bet fraction that maximizes long-run compound growth. It is a hill, not a rising line: bet twice the optimum and long-run growth turns negative even with a genuine edge. Practitioners typically use half of it, retaining about 75% of the growth with a margin for overestimating the edge. See §9.3.
Drawdown回撤 从峰值到谷底的跌幅。回撤与回本的关系是 d ÷ (1−d)，不是对称的：-50% 需要 +100%，-90% 需要 +900%。这条曲线在 -50% 之后近乎垂直，那里存在一条不该越过的线。 The fall from peak to trough. Recovery relates to it as d ÷ (1−d), not symmetrically: −50% needs +100%, −90% needs +900%. The curve is nearly vertical past −50%, and that is a line not to cross.
Leverage杠杆 资产规模与权益之比。它不改变你判断的对错，只放大结果：LTCM 在 26.6:1 杠杆下，资产端约 -3.7% 的走势变成了权益的 -92%。期权本身自带杠杆，再叠加保证金等于杠杆的平方。 Assets divided by equity. It never changes whether you're right, only the magnitude: at LTCM's 26.6:1, a −3.7% move on assets became −92% of equity. Options carry embedded leverage already; adding margin squares it.
BXM / PUTCboe 策略基准指数 Cboe 自 1986 年起计算的期权策略基准：BXM 机械执行"持有标普 500 + 每月卖平值 Call"，PUT 则机械卖出平值 Put。它们是回答"长期到底值不值得做"的唯一不加挑选的证据——但都是指数，不含费用、点差与税。见第 15 章。 Cboe's option-strategy benchmarks, computed since 1986: BXM mechanically runs "hold the S&P 500, sell a one-month ATM call"; PUT mechanically sells ATM puts. They are the only unselected evidence on whether any of this is worth doing long term — but they are indices, excluding fees, spreads and taxes. See Chapter 15.
Sharpe / Sortino Ratio夏普 / 索提诺比率 每承担一单位风险换来多少超额收益；索提诺只把下行波动算作风险。比较两个风险不同的策略时，只看收益率是没有意义的。四十年下来 BXM 与标普的夏普是 0.55 对 0.56——几乎相同，说明降低的风险恰好等价于放弃的收益。 Excess return per unit of risk; Sortino counts only downside volatility as risk. Comparing two strategies of different riskiness on return alone is meaningless. Over forty years BXM and the S&P scored 0.55 against 0.56 — near-identical, meaning the risk avoided was exactly equivalent to the return surrendered.
Regime市场状态 当前市场所处的宏观环境类型（单边牛、震荡、趋势熊、恐慌）。同一策略在不同 Regime 下结果天差地别——先判断状态，再选策略。 The prevailing market environment (one-way bull, chop, trending bear, panic). The same strategy produces wildly different outcomes across regimes — read the regime before choosing the strategy.
