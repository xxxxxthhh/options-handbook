Appendix · Sources & Methodology

来源与方法Sources and Methodology

这是本手册的公开审计台账：它说明哪些内容来自一手文件，哪些是本书的可复算示例，以及哪些网络故事只能当作自述。它是逐章的核心来源映射，不是对每句常识的脚注。 This is the handbook's public audit ledger. It separates primary documents, reproducible book calculations, and internet stories that can only be treated as self-report. It maps each chapter to its core sources; it is not a footnote for every statement of common knowledge.

证据层级Evidence hierarchy

一手来源：监管文件、交易所方法、发行人文件、公司提交文件与学术原文。本书计算：输入在正文公开，由 assets/payoff.js 的唯一定价实现与 tools/checks/ 复算。自述/二手材料：只证明“有人如此声称”，不证明账户、持仓或绩效真实。Primary: regulator records, exchange methodologies, issuer documents, company filings, and original research. Book calculation: inputs are disclosed in the chapter and recomputed through the single pricing implementation in assets/payoff.js and checks under tools/checks/. Self-report/secondary: establishes only that someone made a claim, not that an account, position, or return was independently verified.

可复核规则Reproducibility rules

  1. 历史事实优先链接稳定的发行人、交易所或监管机构 URL；页面访问日期为 2026-08-02。Historical facts prefer stable issuer, exchange, or regulator URLs; pages were last reviewed on 2026-08-02.
  2. 图中的“示意”序列不得被描述为实际行情。精确市场数字若无可公开复现的取数口径，就删除或降级为近似背景。An illustrative chart must never be described as an observed price series. Exact market figures without a publicly reproducible extraction method are removed or reduced to approximate context.
  3. 任何模型输出都必须与实际事实分开；策略反事实不代表历史账户损益。Model outputs are kept separate from observed facts; a strategy counterfactual is not a reconstruction of a historical account.
