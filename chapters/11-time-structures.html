Chapter 11 · The Time Dimension

时间维度：Calendar · Diagonal · PMCCThe Time Dimension: Calendars, Diagonals, and the PMCC

到第 10 章为止，本书所有结构都遵守一条从未言明的规矩：所有腿必须同一个到期日。放开它，你就得到了一个全新的维度——不再只是"押股价走到哪里"，而是"押什么时候走到"。这一章也是全书唯一一处，那条你已经读了十章的实线，不再代表到期时的盈亏。 Through Chapter 10 every structure in this book obeyed an unstated rule: all legs share one expiration. Drop it and a new dimension opens — no longer just betting on where the stock goes, but on when it gets there. This is also the one chapter in the book where the solid line you have been reading for ten chapters no longer means P/L at expiry.

11.1 放开最后一个约束11.1 Dropping the Last Constraint

第 4 章的垂直价差改变了 Strike，第 5 章的组合改变了腿的数量和方向。但它们都共享同一个到期日，所以第 3 章那四个仪表盘可以整体读数：整个头寸有一个 Theta、一个 Vega。一旦两条腿到期日不同，这个前提就崩了——近月腿和远月腿的 Theta 衰减速度不同、对 IV 的敏感度也不同，而这个差异本身就成了可以交易的东西。 Chapter 4's verticals varied the strike; Chapter 5's structures varied the number and direction of legs. All shared one expiry, so Chapter 3's four gauges could be read for the position as a whole: one theta, one vega. The moment two legs expire on different dates, that premise breaks — the near and far legs decay at different speeds and respond differently to IV, and that difference itself becomes tradable.

回看第 3 章那张 Theta 衰减图：ATM 期权从 90 天走到 30 天只损失约 44% 的价值，而从 30 天走到 0 要损失剩下的全部。衰减是加速的——这意味着近月和远月在"每天损失多少"上存在系统性差异，而差异是可以被套利的。卖出衰减快的，买入衰减慢的，这就是本章所有结构的共同内核。 Look again at Chapter 3's decay curve: an ATM option loses about 44% of its value going from 90 days to 30, then all of the remainder going from 30 to zero. Decay accelerates — which means near and far months differ systematically in how much they shed per day, and a systematic difference can be harvested. Sell the one decaying fast, buy the one decaying slowly. That is the shared core of every structure in this chapter.

卖出一张 30 天的 Call，同时买入一张同 Strike 的 60 天 Call。近月衰减快、远月衰减慢，所以只要股价老实待在 Strike 附近，你卖掉的那条腿会先归零，而你买的那条腿还剩着大半价值。用本章的示意参数（现价 $100、Strike $100、近月 IV 35%、远月 IV 28%）：净借记只要 $0.69，而近月到期时若股价正好在 $100，头寸值 $2.68——相对成本约 390%。 Sell a 30-day call and buy a 60-day call at the same strike. The near leg decays fast, the far leg slowly, so if the stock sits near the strike, the leg you sold goes to zero while the leg you own still holds most of its value. With this chapter's illustrative inputs (spot $100, strike $100, near IV 35%, far IV 28%) the net debit is just $0.69, and if the stock is exactly $100 when the near leg expires the position is worth $2.68 — about 390% on cost.

前十章里，实线的含义始终固定：到期那一刻的盈亏，此时所有时间价值都已归零，所以那条线一定由直线段和折点组成。上面这张图的实线是拱形的，因为它画的是近月腿到期那一刻——而此时远月腿还活着，还剩 30 天的时间价值。那不是"到期盈亏图"，那是一个中途快照。 For ten chapters the solid line meant one fixed thing: P/L at expiry, when all time value has gone to zero — which is why it was always made of straight segments and kinks. The solid line above is curved, because it shows the moment the near leg expires, while the far leg is still alive with 30 days of time value left. That is not an expiry payoff diagram; it is a mid-flight snapshot.

这有一个直接的实际后果：那个 $2.68 的峰值不是你能锁定的结果，而是一个估值。它取决于远月腿在那一刻的 IV 是多少——而没有人能在开仓时知道这个数字。前十章的最大收益是合同保证的，这一章的不是。图中的"最大收益"读数，是在假设远月 IV 保持不变下算出来的，这个假设几乎一定不成立。下一节就是关于它为什么不成立。 This has a direct practical consequence: that $2.68 peak is not a result you can lock in — it is a valuation. It depends on the far leg's IV at that moment, a number nobody knows at entry. In the first ten chapters, maximum gain was contractually guaranteed; here it is not. The chart's "max gain" readout is computed assuming the far leg's IV is unchanged — an assumption that is almost never true. The next section is about why.

11.3 期限结构：近月为什么有时更贵11.3 Term Structure: When the Near Month Costs More

第 3 章讲了不同 Strike 的 IV 不同（Skew），第 10 章解释了为什么平价关系允许这件事。现在是另一个方向：同一个 Strike、不同到期日的 IV 也不同，这条曲线叫 Term Structure（期限结构）。 Chapter 3 showed IV differs across strikes (skew), and Chapter 10 explained why parity permits it. Now the other axis: IV also differs across expiries at the same strike. That curve is the term structure.

TERM STRUCTURE （典型形态示意）(illustrative shapes)

平静时期曲线向上倾斜（contango）：远月不确定性更多，所以 IV 更高。这是常态——第 8 章说过，VIX 期货长期处于这个状态，那正是 XIV 能连年上涨的结构性来源。而在恐慌中曲线会整个翻转：所有人都在抢购最近的保护，近月 IV 冲到远月之上，曲线变成向下倾斜（backwardation，贴水）。2020 年 2 月下旬到 3 月就是教科书式的例子——自 2006 年以来，VIX 曲线只有约 19% 的交易日处于倒挂状态，且主要集中在 2008–09、2020 年 3 月这样的危机时段。 In calm markets the curve slopes up (contango): more time means more uncertainty, so higher IV. This is the normal state — Chapter 8 noted that VIX futures live in it, which is the structural source of XIV's years of gains. In a panic the curve inverts wholesale: everyone bids for the nearest protection, near-dated IV vaults above far-dated, and the curve slopes down (backwardation). Late February into March 2020 is the textbook instance — since 2006 the VIX curve has been inverted on only about 19% of trading days, mostly clustered in crisis stretches such as 2008–09 and March 2020.

CASE FILE · 期限结构倒挂an inverted curve

恐慌中的日历价差：便宜得可疑A Calendar in a Panic: Suspiciously Cheap

曲线倒挂时，日历价差看起来是白送的：你卖出的近月 IV 高达 75%，买入的远月只有 55%。用引擎按这组示意 IV 计算，净借记压缩到只要 $0.47（同一结构在 11.2 的平静 IV 下要 $0.69），而如果股价到期时停在 Strike，头寸值 $5.97——账面回报约 1,277%。任何筛选器都会把它标成绝佳机会。 With the curve inverted, a calendar looks free: the near leg you sell carries 75% IV, the far leg you buy only 55%. On those illustrative IVs the engine puts the net debit at just $0.47 (against $0.69 for the same structure at §11.2's calm-market IVs), and if the stock pins the strike the position is worth $5.97 — a headline of roughly 1,277%. Any screener would flag it as an outstanding opportunity.

但这个数字建立在一个隐藏假设上：远月的 IV 一直保持 55%。而恐慌之所以是恐慌，就在于它会结束。把远月 IV 从 55% 调回常态的 25%，同一个头寸的账面峰值从 $5.97 塌到 $2.55；更要命的是两侧的盈亏——股价上下 15% 时，原本还赚 $1.53 和 $0.72 的两个点位，双双变成亏损（−$0.07 和 −$0.44）。 But that figure rests on a hidden assumption: the far leg's IV stays at 55%. And what makes a panic a panic is that it ends. Return the far leg's IV to a normal 25% and the same position's peak collapses from $5.97 to $2.55 — and worse, the wings flip: at ±15% the position went from making $1.53 and $0.72 to losing $0.07 and $0.44.

请自己把上面那张图的"远月当前 IV"滑块从 55% 拖到 25%，看着那条实线整体下沉、两侧翻负。这就是恐慌期日历价差的真实处境：你需要股价一动不动，而这恰恰是恐慌中最不可能发生的事；同时你还在赌远月 IV 不会回落，而它几乎一定会回落。两个条件都必须成立，而它们互相矛盾。 Drag the "far-leg IV now" slider on the chart above from 55% down to 25% and watch the solid line sink and its wings go negative. This is the real situation of a panic calendar: you need the stock to sit perfectly still — the least likely thing in a panic — while simultaneously betting the far leg's IV won't normalise, which it almost certainly will. Both conditions must hold, and they contradict each other.

日历价差真正的用武之地Where calendars genuinely belong

上面的分析不是说日历价差没用，而是说它的适用条件比看上去窄得多。它真正的舒适区是：近月有一个已知的、会自行消失的事件溢价，而远月没有——最典型的就是财报。卖出财报周的合约、买入财报后的合约，你卖掉的是第 6 章那块"结构性必然蒸发"的事件溢价，而买入的那条腿几乎不含这块溢价。这是一个有明确到期日的信息优势，而不是对 IV 走向的猜测。与恐慌期的倒挂相比，这个场景里"近月贵"的原因是已知且有日期的。 None of that says calendars are useless — it says their window is much narrower than it looks. Their genuine comfort zone is this: the near month carries a known event premium that will destroy itself, and the far month doesn't — earnings being the archetype. Sell the contract expiring in earnings week and buy the one after, and what you sold is Chapter 6's structurally-doomed event premium while the leg you bought barely contains it. That is an informational edge with a date attached, not a guess about where IV is heading. Unlike a panic inversion, here the reason the near month is expensive is known and calendared.

11.4 Diagonal 与 PMCC：把两个维度一起用11.4 Diagonals and the PMCC: Using Both Axes at Once

既然可以改 Strike（垂直），也可以改到期日（水平），那就没有理由不同时改——这就是 Diagonal Spread（对角价差）。它最流行的形态有一个很直白的名字：Poor Man's Covered Call（穷人的备兑看涨，简称 PMCC）。 If you can vary the strike (vertical) and the expiry (horizontal), nothing stops you doing both at once — that is a diagonal spread. Its most popular form has a blunt name: the poor man's covered call, or PMCC.

做法：不买 100 股正股，而是买入一张深度价内的长期 Call（LEAPS）当作股票的替身，然后照常每月卖出价外 Call 收租。因为第 10 章已经证明"深度价内 Call ≈ 正股"，这个替换在形状上是成立的。省下的资金非常可观： Instead of buying 100 shares you buy one deep-ITM long-dated call (LEAPS) as a stock substitute, then sell monthly OTM calls against it as usual. Since Chapter 10 established that a deep-ITM call behaves like the stock, the substitution works in shape. The capital saved is dramatic:

同样 $33,236：Covered Call 亏 $10,619 · PMCC 亏 $19,735 Same $33,236: covered call −$10,619 · PMCC −$19,735

同样的资金、同一个看多判断、同一只股票、同一天——PMCC 的亏损是传统 Covered Call 的 1.86 倍。原因是第 10 章那个等价关系有个前提：深度价内 Call 只有在保持深度价内时才像股票。$250 的 Call 在 NFLX 报 $348 时 Delta 约 0.83，大体上像一只股票；股价砸到 $226 后它变成价外，Delta 塌到约 0.55——一夜之间，你持有的从"一只股票的替身"变成了"半只股票加一叠会衰减的时间价值"。它的价值从 $120.67 砍到 $42.44（−65%），远深于正股的 −35%。省下的 74% 资金不是免费的，它是杠杆，而杠杆在两个方向上同样忠实。 Same capital, same bullish view, same stock, same day — and the PMCC lost 1.86× what the covered call did. The reason is that Chapter 10's equivalence carries a precondition: a deep-ITM call behaves like stock only while it stays deep ITM. At $348 the $250 call had a delta of about 0.83 and behaved much like the stock; after the drop to $226 it was out of the money with delta down to about 0.55 — overnight, what you held went from "a substitute for a stock" to "half a stock plus a stack of decaying time value." Its price was cut from $120.67 to $42.44 (−65%), far deeper than the stock's −35%. The 74% of capital you saved was never free — it was leverage, and leverage is equally faithful in both directions.

期限结构倒挂（近月 IV 高于远月）时买入日历价差，主要风险是什么？Buying a calendar while the term structure is inverted (near IV above far) carries what main risk?

B。两个条件必须同时成立，而它们互相矛盾：日历价差要求股价停在 Strike 附近，而期限结构倒挂本身就是市场剧烈波动的信号。此外，本章用引擎演示过，把远月 IV 从 55% 调回 25%，峰值从 $5.97 塌到 $2.55，±15% 两个点位从盈利翻成亏损。A 正是这个陷阱的诱饵——约 1,277% 的账面回报建立在"远月 IV 不回落"这个几乎必然不成立的假设上。 B. Two conditions must both hold and they contradict each other: a calendar needs the stock pinned near the strike, while an inverted term structure is itself the signal that the market is convulsing. Beyond that, this chapter demonstrated with the engine that returning far IV from 55% to 25% collapses the peak from $5.97 to $2.55 and flips both ±15% points from profit to loss. A is precisely the bait: that roughly-1,277% headline rests on "far IV won't fall," which it almost certainly will.

PMCC 用 $2,635 替代了 $10,000 的股票仓位，收取同样的租金。这意味着什么？A PMCC replaces a $10,000 share position with $2,635 and collects the same rent. That means:

D。NFLX 案例给了确切数字：同样 $33,236 的资金，PMCC 亏 $19,735，传统 Covered Call 亏 $10,619——1.86 倍。A 混淆了"占用资金少"和"风险小"，这正是本章思考题要拆穿的：换分母不等于降风险。B 忽略了第 10 章那个等价关系的前提——深度价内 Call 只在保持深度价内时才像股票，NFLX 一夜跌 35.1% 后它的 Delta 从约 0.83 塌到约 0.55、价值从 $120.67 跌到 $42.44——替身只剩半只股票，杠杆显形。 D. The NFLX case gives the exact figures: on the same $33,236, the PMCC lost $19,735 against the covered call's $10,619 — 1.86×. A confuses "ties up less capital" with "is less risky," which is exactly what this chapter's Think question dismantles: changing the denominator is not reducing risk. B ignores the precondition on Chapter 10's equivalence — a deep-ITM call behaves like stock only while it stays deep ITM, and after NFLX fell 35.1% overnight its delta went from about 0.83 to about 0.55 and its price from $120.67 to $42.44 — the substitute was down to half a stock, and the leverage showed itself.

日历价差最合理的应用场景是？The most defensible use case for a calendar spread is:

A。近月贵的原因必须是已知的、有日期的、且会自行消失的——财报事件溢价正好三条全中（第 6 章证明过它的蒸发是结构性的）。B 的倒挂虽然也让近月更贵，但原因是市场在剧烈波动，而那正好摧毁日历价差需要的"股价不动"。C 更糟：趋势行情直接把股价推离 Strike。D 混淆了"最大亏损有限"和"这笔交易划算"——第 4 章已经说过，定义风险不等于低风险。 A. The near month's richness must be known, dated, and self-destructing — earnings event premium is all three (Chapter 6 showed its evaporation is structural). B's inversion also makes the near month expensive, but the reason is a convulsing market, which destroys the stillness a calendar requires. C is worse: a trend drives the stock away from the strike. D confuses "the maximum loss is bounded" with "this trade is worth taking" — Chapter 4 already established that defined risk is not low risk.

为什么日历价差的"最大收益"不像垂直价差那样是一个确定的数字？Why isn't a calendar's "maximum gain" a definite number the way a vertical's is?

C。垂直价差的最大收益是"宽度 − 净借记"，两项在开仓时都已确定，是合同保证的。日历价差的峰值发生在近月到期、远月仍存活的那一刻，其价值必须用那一刻的远月 IV 折算——而没有人能提前知道它。本章的演示就是证据：仅仅把远月 IV 从 55% 改成 25%，"最大收益"就从 $5.97 变成 $2.55。D 说的是所有策略的共性，答不到点上：垂直价差同样无法预测股价，但它的最大收益依然是确定的。 C. A vertical's maximum gain is "width − net debit," both fixed at entry and contractually guaranteed. A calendar's peak occurs when the near leg expires and the far leg lives on, so its value must be marked at the far leg's IV at that moment — which nobody knows in advance. This chapter's demonstration is the proof: changing far IV from 55% to 25% alone moves "maximum gain" from $5.97 to $2.55. D states something true of every strategy and misses the point: a vertical's stock price is equally unpredictable, yet its maximum gain is still definite.

本章要点Key Takeaways

放开"所有腿同一到期日"这条约束，交易的对象就从"股价走到哪里"变成了"时间价值在两个日期之间的差异"。这带来了本书唯一一次阅读约定的破例——而且只发生在本章这两张图上：日历价差与 PMCC 的实线都不是到期盈亏图，而是短腿到期那一刻的估值快照——它是拱形的，因为远月腿还活着，而那也意味着它的"最大收益"不是合同保证的数字，只是一个依赖远月 IV 假设的估值。把远月 IV 从 55% 调到 25%，峰值就从 $5.97 塌到 $2.55，两侧从盈利翻成亏损。所以恐慌中那个约 1,277% 的账面回报是个陷阱：它要求股价一动不动，而期限结构倒挂本身就是市场在剧烈波动的证据；日历价差真正的舒适区是财报这种已知的、有日期的、会自行蒸发的近月溢价。至于 PMCC，它的资金效率完全真实——同样的租金只要四分之一的钱——但 NFLX 在 2022 年 4 月 20 日一夜下跌 35.1% 给出了另一半账单：同样 $33,236 的资金、同一个看多判断，PMCC 亏 $19,735，传统 Covered Call 亏 $10,619。深度价内 Call 只在保持深度价内时才像股票，而"省下的资金"从来不是效率，它是杠杆。 Drop the "one expiry for all legs" constraint and what you trade stops being where the stock goes and becomes the difference in time value between two dates. That brings the book's only break in its own reading convention, confined to this chapter's two charts: neither the calendar's nor the PMCC's solid line is an expiry payoff — each is a valuation snapshot at the short leg's expiry — curved, because the far leg is still alive, which also means its "maximum gain" is not a guaranteed number but an estimate resting on an assumption about far-leg IV. Move that IV from 55% to 25% and the peak falls from $5.97 to $2.55 while both wings flip from profit to loss. So the roughly-1,277% headline in a panic is a trap: it requires the stock to sit perfectly still, and an inverted term structure is itself evidence the market is convulsing. A calendar's genuine comfort zone is a near-month premium that is known, dated and self-destructing — earnings. As for the PMCC, its capital efficiency is entirely real — the same rent for a quarter of the money — but Netflix's 35.1% overnight fall on April 20, 2022 presented the other half of the bill: on the same $33,236 and the same bullish view, the PMCC lost $19,735 against the covered call's $10,619. A deep-ITM call resembles stock only while it stays deep ITM, and the capital you "saved" was never efficiency — it was leverage.
