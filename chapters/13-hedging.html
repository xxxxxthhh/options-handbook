Chapter 13 · Hedging Engineering

对冲的工程学：尾部保险的成本核算Hedging Engineering: Costing Out Tail Protection

第 2 章说 Protective Put 是"特定时期的战术工具，不是常态配置"，并让你自己去算连续买保险的年化成本。这一章把那笔账算完。结论会让很多人不舒服：在常态市场里永久对冲，一年要花掉约 8.8%——而标普 500 四十年的年化总回报是 11.2%。 Chapter 2 called the protective put "a tactical tool for specific windows, not a permanent allocation," and left you to work out what continuous insurance costs per year. This chapter finishes that calculation. The answer is uncomfortable: permanently hedging in a normal market costs about 8.8% a year — against the S&P 500's 11.2% annualized total return over forty years.

13.1 先把账单摆出来13.1 The Bill, Up Front

最简单的持续对冲方案：每月买入一张 5% 价外的 Put，到期后再买下一张，永不间断。用本书的引擎按不同的市场波动水平定价（偏斜使价外 Put 的 IV 高于平值，此处统一加 3 个百分点）： The simplest continuous hedge: buy a 5% out-of-the-money put every month, roll it at expiry, never stop. 把这张表和第 15 章那个四十年的数字放在一起，结论就出来了：标普 500 自 1986 年以来的年化总回报是 11.2%。在常态波动水平下永久对冲，每年要交出 8.8%——也就是股权溢价的绝大部分。这不是"对冲很贵"这种模糊说法，这是一道减法。 Set that table beside Chapter 15's forty-year figure and the conclusion writes itself: the S&P 500 has returned 11.2% annualized since 1986. Permanently hedging at normal volatility surrenders 8.8% of that every year — very nearly the whole equity premium. This isn't the vague claim that "hedging is expensive"; it is a subtraction.

而且它在你最需要的时候最贵And it costs most exactly when you want it most

注意这张表的形状：成本不是恒定的，它随波动率一起上升——从平静时的 4.5% 到恐慌时的 37.2%，相差八倍多。这意味着一件很残酷的事：当市场平静、你觉得不需要保险时，保险最便宜；当市场已经开始崩塌、你终于想买保险时，它贵了八倍。第 3 章用 2024 年 8 月那次套息平仓演示过同一件事（提前三天买，成本低 39%），只是这次我们看到的是整条曲线。"等出事了再对冲"在算术上是不可行的，不是因为纪律问题，而是因为价格。 Note the shape of that table: the cost is not constant, it rises with volatility — from 4.5% in calm to 37.2% in panic, a factor of more than eight. Which implies something brutal: when markets are quiet and you feel no need for insurance, insurance is cheap; when markets are already breaking and you finally want it, it costs eight times as much. Chapter 3 demonstrated the same thing with the August 2024 carry unwind (three days earlier, 39% cheaper) — this time you can see the whole curve. "Hedge once trouble starts" is arithmetically unavailable, not for want of discipline but because of the price.

13.2 三个把账单压下来的杠杆13.2 Three Levers That Lower the Bill

既然 8.8% 交不起，工程问题就变成了：怎么在保留有意义保护的前提下，把这个数字压到可承受的范围。只有三个杠杆，而它们都在前面的章节里出现过。 Since 8.8% is unaffordable, the engineering question becomes: how do you compress that number into something bearable while retaining meaningful protection? There are only three levers, and every one has appeared in an earlier chapter.

① 买得更远① Further OTM
便宜得多，但只保尾部Far cheaper, tail only
② 卖掉一部分② Sell some back
价差 / 领口（第 4、10 章）Spreads / collars (Ch.4, 10)
③ 只在便宜时买③ Buy only when cheap
IV 分位过滤（第 5 章）IV rank filter (Ch.5)

第 ① 条是尾部对冲的核心思路：不再保护"下跌 5% 以后的全部损失"，而只保护"下跌 20% 以后的部分"。深度价外的 Put 便宜得多，代价是绝大多数回撤它根本不赔——它只在真正的崩盘里兑现。第 ② 条是第 10 章 JHEQX 的做法：卖出上行和更深的下行来补贴保费，代价是封顶与保护失效区间。第 ③ 条把对冲从"永久配置"变回"战术动作"，也就是第 2 章一开始说的那句话。 Lever ① is the essence of tail hedging: stop insuring "everything below −5%" and insure only "everything below −20%." Deep OTM puts are far cheaper, and the price is that they pay nothing in the overwhelming majority of drawdowns — they only fire in an actual crash. Lever ② is JHEQX's approach from Chapter 10: sell upside and deeper downside to subsidise the premium, at the cost of a cap and a protection gap. Lever ③ turns hedging from a permanent allocation back into a tactical action — which is exactly what Chapter 2 said in the first place.

读懂"+4,144%"这种数字Reading a "+4,144%" headline

尾部对冲最著名的例子是 Universa Investments（Nassim Taleb 任顾问）的 Black Swan Protection Protocol：据多家媒体报道，该基金 2020 年 3 月单月回报约 +3,612%、一季度约 +4,144%，而同期标普 500 下跌约 20%。这个数字是真实报道的，但它几乎无法直接使用。 Tail hedging's most famous instance is Universa Investments (advised by Nassim Taleb) and its Black Swan Protection Protocol: multiple outlets reported roughly +3,612% for March 2020 and about +4,144% for the first quarter, against an S&P 500 down some 20%. The figure is genuinely reported — and almost unusable as it stands.

原因是第 15 章那三个问题里的第一个：分母是什么？这类回报通常按投入资本（实际花出去的权利金）计算，而不是按组合规模。尾部对冲的设计前提就是只占组合很小的一个切片，所以"+4,144%"和"你的组合涨了 41 倍"之间隔着一个你不知道的分母。在你能说出那个分母之前，这个数字不能用来做任何配置决策——它说明的是策略的形状（极度凸性），不是它对你组合的贡献。 The reason is the first of Chapter 15's three questions: what is the denominator? Returns like this are typically quoted on invested capital — the premium actually spent — not on portfolio size. Tail hedging is designed to occupy a small sleeve, so between "+4,144%" and "your portfolio multiplied by 41" sits a denominator you have not been told. Until you can state that denominator the number cannot inform any allocation decision — it describes the strategy's shape (extreme convexity), not its contribution to your portfolio.

13.3 不要用会衰减的工具做长期保险13.3 Never Use a Decaying Instrument as Long-Term Insurance

既然买 Put 这么贵、又要不停展期，一个自然的想法是：能不能直接买一个"做多波动率"的 ETF 拿着？它看起来省事得多——不用挑行权价、不用管到期日、像股票一样买入即可。 Given how expensive puts are and how relentlessly they must be rolled, a natural thought: why not just buy a long-volatility ETF and hold it? It looks far simpler — no strike to choose, no expiry to manage, buy it like a stock.

CASE FILE · 2009–2026

VXX 的十七年：VIX 跌了 65%，它跌了 98%Seventeen Years of VXX: the VIX Fell 65%, It Fell 98%

VXX 是最主流的做多短期波动率工具，2009 年 1 月 29 日上市。自上市至今，VXX 累计下跌约 98%，而同期VIX 本身只下跌约 65%。这中间 33 个百分点的差额，不是因为看错了波动率方向——是因为这个工具本身在持续漏水。 VXX is the mainstream long-short-term-volatility vehicle, listed on January 29, 2009. Since listing, VXX is down roughly 98%, while the VIX itself is down only about 65%. The 33-point gap is not the result of a wrong call on volatility — it is the vehicle itself leaking.

漏水的原因第 8 章已经讲过，只是那次是从获利的一侧看的：VIX 期货长期处于 contango（远月比近月贵）。VXX 每天卖出即将到期的近月、买入更贵的次月，每一次展期都在高买低卖。第 8 章里，XIV 靠站在这个结构的反面连年上涨——那么站在正面的 VXX 就必然连年下跌。它们是同一枚硬币：XIV 一夜之间蒸发 96%，VXX 用十七年慢慢蒸发 98%。 The leak's cause appeared in Chapter 8, seen from the profitable side: VIX futures sit in persistent contango, later months priced above nearer ones. VXX sells the expiring near month daily and buys the pricier next one — every roll is buying high and selling low. In Chapter 8, XIV rose year after year by standing on the other side of exactly this structure; so VXX must fall year after year. They are one coin: XIV evaporated 96% in a night, VXX evaporated 98% over seventeen years.

13.4 那么什么时候对冲是理性的13.4 So When Is Hedging Rational?

如果永久对冲要交出几乎全部股权溢价，而第 15 章又证明卖方策略在风险调整后并不占便宜，那么一个诚实的问题是：对冲到底还有什么用？ If permanent hedging surrenders nearly the entire equity premium, and Chapter 15 shows selling strategies gain nothing risk-adjusted, an honest question follows: what is hedging for at all?

答案不在期望值里，在路径里。第 9 章那条回撤曲线说过：−50% 需要 +100% 才能回本，而这个算术对一个必须在特定时间用钱的人是致命的。如果你还有三十年才退休，回撤只是波动；如果你明年要用这笔钱，同样的回撤是永久性损失。对冲不改变期望收益——它把"什么时候需要用钱"这个约束，翻译成了组合可以承受的形状。 The answer isn't in expectancy, it's in the path. Chapter 9's drawdown curve established that −50% requires +100% to recover, and that arithmetic is lethal to anyone who must spend the money on a particular date. Thirty years from retirement, a drawdown is volatility; if you need the money next year, the same drawdown is a permanent loss. Hedging doesn't change expected return — it translates the constraint "when do I need this money" into a shape the portfolio can survive.

思考Think 13.1 那张表说明：保险的价格随波动率上升八倍，而波动率恰好在市场下跌时上升。这意味着一个自洽的对冲计划，必须在你最不想花钱的时候花钱——市场平静、组合创新高、所有人都在赚钱的那几个月。如果一个策略要求你在感觉最好的时候持续付费、在感觉最糟的时候什么都不做，它失败的原因会是数学问题，还是别的什么？把这个问题和第 9 章"连续成功会放大仓位"放在一起想。 §13.1's table shows insurance prices rise eightfold with volatility, and volatility rises precisely when markets fall. Which means a coherent hedging plan must spend money exactly when you least want to — in the calm months when the portfolio is at highs and everyone is making money. If a strategy requires you to keep paying when you feel best and to do nothing when you feel worst, will its failures be mathematical or something else? Consider it alongside Chapter 9's observation that a run of success inflates position size.

本章要点Key Takeaways

第 2 章留下的那道作业，答案是这样的：每月滚动买入 5% 价外 Put，平静时年化 4.5%，常态 8.8%，紧张 19.3%，恐慌 37.2%。把 8.8% 放在标普四十年 11.2% 的年化总回报旁边，永久对冲交出的几乎就是全部股权溢价——这不是"保险很贵"的感慨，是一道减法。更要命的是这条曲线的形状：成本随波动率上升八倍，而波动率恰好在你想买保险时上升，所以"等出事了再对冲"在算术上就不可行。压低账单只有三个杠杆：买得更远（尾部对冲）、卖掉一部分（价差与领口）、只在 IV 分位低时买（把对冲变回战术动作）。至于那些看起来更省事的工具——VXX 十七年跌了约 98%，而同期 VIX 只跌 65%，差额全部来自 contango 展期损耗，折合年化约 −20.6%，比自己滚动买 Put 还贵一倍以上，而且它不是合约、不保证赔付。它和第 8 章那个一夜蒸发 96% 的 XIV 是同一枚硬币的两面。而所有这些数字合起来指向一个不在期望值里的结论：对冲不改变你的期望收益，它只把"我什么时候必须用这笔钱"这个约束，翻译成组合能够承受的形状——所以真正决定要不要对冲的，从来不是你对市场的预测，而是你资金的期限。 Chapter 2's homework has an answer: rolling a monthly 5%-OTM put costs 4.5% a year in calm, 8.8% in normal conditions, 19.3% when tense and 37.2% in panic. Put 8.8% beside the S&P's 11.2% forty-year annualized return and permanent hedging hands back very nearly the entire equity premium — not a lament that insurance is dear, but a subtraction. Worse is the shape of the curve: cost rises eightfold with volatility, and volatility rises exactly when you want the insurance, which makes "hedge once trouble starts" arithmetically unavailable. Only three levers compress the bill: buy further out (tail hedging), sell part of it back (spreads and collars), and buy only at low IV rank (returning hedging to a tactical act). As for the vehicles that look easier — VXX has fallen about 98% in seventeen years while the VIX fell only 65%, the entire gap being contango roll drag, about −20.6% annualized, more than twice the cost of rolling puts yourself, and it is not a contract and guarantees no payout. It is the same coin as Chapter 8's XIV, which evaporated 96% in a single night. Together these numbers point at a conclusion that lives outside expectancy: hedging does not change your expected return; it translates the constraint "when must I spend this money" into a shape your portfolio can survive — so what decides whether to hedge was never your forecast of the market, but the horizon of your capital.
