Chapter 13 · Hedging Engineering

对冲的工程学：尾部保险的成本核算Hedging Engineering: Costing Out Tail Protection

第 2 章说 Protective Put 是“特定时期的战术工具，不是常态配置”，并留下一个预算问题：连续买保险可能需要多大的毛支出？本章先做一个明确受限的情景计算。在固定标的、30 天到期、VIX≈20 与指定偏斜等静态输入下，单月模型保费乘以 12 得到 8.8% 的年化毛支出情景——它与标普 500 四十年 11.2% 的年化总回报处在同一个量级，但不是实际滚动策略的年成本。 Chapter 2 called the protective put “a tactical tool for specific windows, not a permanent allocation,” and left a budgeting question: how large might the gross outlay for continuous insurance be? This chapter starts with an explicitly bounded scenario. With spot fixed, 30-day maturity, VIX≈20, and the stated skew inputs, one model month's premium × 12 produces an 8.8% annualized gross-outlay scenario — the same order of magnitude as the S&P 500's 11.2% annualized total return over forty years, but not the annual cost of an actual rolling strategy.

13.1 先把账单摆出来13.1 The Bill, Up Front

最简单的持续对冲方案：每月买入一张 5% 价外的 Put，到期后再买下一张，永不间断。用本书的引擎按不同的市场波动水平定价（偏斜使价外 Put 的 IV 高于平值，此处统一加 3 个百分点）： The simplest continuous hedge: buy a 5% out-of-the-money put every month, roll it at expiry, never stop. 先说清楚这张表算的是什么：它是毛保费支出——你每年为这些 Put 付出去的钱，还没有减去它们的赔付。在指数单月跌破行权价时，Put 的赔付会抵消部分乃至超过累计保费，因此净拖累可能远低于毛支出。本章没有运行逐月、含展期与赔付的历史回测，所以不能给出净拖累的数值或方向。年度收益为正或负也不能推断月度尾部赔付；要回答这个问题，必须使用逐月路径、实际行权价、当时 IV、成交成本和赔付。 First, be clear what that table measures: it is gross premium outlay — what you pay each year for the puts, before subtracting what they pay back. When the index finishes a month below the strike, payouts offset part of the premium and can even exceed cumulative outlay, so net drag may be far below gross spending. This chapter does not run a monthly historical backtest with rolls and payouts, so it cannot state the amount or even the sign of net drag. Positive or negative annual returns do not identify monthly tail payouts; answering that question requires the monthly path, actual strikes, contemporaneous IV, execution costs, and payouts.

所以正确的说法不是“11.2% 减 8.8%”这样一道减法，而是：在这组静态模型输入下，8.8% 是值得进入预算压力测试的毛支出量级。它与标普长期 11.2% 年化总回报的并列只能提醒你成本可能很大，不能推出对冲后的期望收益、净拖累或风险调整结果。这些结论必须由完整净损益回测支持，而本章没有提供这样的回测。 So the correct statement is not the subtraction “11.2 minus 8.8.” It is this: under these static model inputs, 8.8% is a gross-outlay magnitude that belongs in a budget stress test. Placing it beside the S&P's 11.2% long-run annualized total return only signals that the spending may be material; it does not establish hedged expected return, net drag, or risk-adjusted performance. Those claims require a complete net-P/L backtest, which this chapter does not provide.

这张表的计算口径How the table is computed

每月保费由本书引擎按 Black-Scholes 计算（标的指数化为 100、30 天到期、利率 4%、价外 Put 的 IV 在平值基础上加 3 个百分点以反映偏斜），年化情景值 = 单月保费 × 12。这一步隐含两个简化：① 假设十二次买入都发生在标的价格不变（100）的水平上，真实市场里标的会移动、每次的 5% 价外行权价也随之改变；② 只计保费支出、不计赔付，所以它描述的是这组固定输入下的毛支出情景，不是净成本。它也不是实际毛支出的上界：真实 IV、偏斜、路径和成交价格都可能令全年保费高于或低于这个静态数值。这些数字只用于展示量级及其随 IV 变化的形状，不是任何具体对冲方案的回测结果。 Each month's premium is a Black-Scholes value from this book's engine (index normalised to 100, 30 days, 4% rate, with 3 points added to the OTM put's IV for skew), and the annualized scenario is one month's premium × 12. That step embeds two simplifications: ① it assumes all twelve purchases occur with the underlying unchanged at 100, whereas in a real market the index moves and each 5%-OTM strike moves with it; ② it counts premium outflows only, never payouts, so it is a gross-outlay scenario under fixed inputs, not a net-cost estimate. Nor is it an upper bound on actual gross spending: realized IV, skew, path, and execution prices can make annual premiums higher or lower than this static figure. The numbers show magnitude and sensitivity to IV; they are not a backtest of any specific hedging programme.

而且它在你最需要的时候最贵And it costs most exactly when you want it most

注意这张情景表的形状：在其他输入不变时，Put 毛保费随 IV 上升——从 IV≈15 情景的 4.5% 到 IV≈45 情景的 37.2%，相差八倍多。这不是说压力中“无法”买保险，而是说当压力已被 IV 定价后，同样规则的保护可能需要完全不同的预算。第 3 章的 2024 年 8 月示意也展示了时点对保费的敏感性。是否此时仍值得买，取决于剩余风险、价格与约束，不能只凭“已经出事”决定。 Note the shape of the scenario table: holding other inputs fixed, gross put premium rises with IV — from 4.5% in the IV≈15 case to 37.2% in the IV≈45 case, more than eightfold. That does not make insurance “unavailable” during stress; it means once stress is priced into IV, the same protection rule may require a completely different budget. Chapter 3's August 2024 illustration also shows premium sensitivity to timing. Whether buying then is still worthwhile depends on residual risk, price, and constraints, not on “trouble has started” alone.

13.2 三个把账单压下来的杠杆13.2 Three Levers That Lower the Bill

如果这组输入下的 8.8% 毛支出超过预算，工程问题就变成了：怎么在保留有意义保护的前提下，把毛支出压到可承受的范围。下面是三个常见杠杆；每个都要用完整净损益验证。 If the 8.8% gross outlay under these inputs exceeds the budget, the engineering question becomes: how can gross spending be reduced while retaining meaningful protection? Three common levers follow, and each needs validation on complete net P/L.

① 买得更远① Further OTM
便宜得多，但只保尾部Far cheaper, tail only
② 卖掉一部分② Sell some back
价差 / 领口（第 4、10 章）Spreads / collars (Ch.4, 10)
③ 有条件部署③ Conditional deployment
IV 分位可作候选输入，非已证实信号IV rank may be a candidate input, not a proven signal

第 ① 条是尾部对冲的核心思路：不再保护“下跌 5% 以后的全部损失”，而只保护“下跌 20% 以后的部分”。深度价外 Put 的初始保费通常更低，代价是许多回撤没有到期赔付。第 ② 条对应第 10 章发行人公开的 Put-spread Collar 方法：卖出上行和更深的下行来补贴保费，同时引入封顶与保护失效区间。第 ③ 条只是待检验假设：低 IV 分位不保证保护“便宜”或随后下跌，必须与常态配置和不对冲方案做样本外、扣费后的完整净损益比较。 Lever ① is the essence of tail hedging: stop insuring “everything below −5%” and insure only “everything below −20%.” Deep OTM puts usually have lower initial premium, at the cost of no expiry payout in many drawdowns. Lever ② corresponds to Chapter 10's issuer-published put-spread collar method: sell upside and deeper downside to subsidize premium while adding a cap and a protection gap. Lever ③ is only a hypothesis to test: low IV rank does not guarantee that protection is “cheap” or that a decline follows; compare it out of sample and after costs against always-on and unhedged alternatives using complete net P/L.

13.3 不要用会衰减的工具做长期保险13.3 Never Use a Decaying Instrument as Long-Term Insurance

既然买 Put 这么贵、又要不停展期，一个自然的想法是：能不能直接买一个"做多波动率"的 ETF 拿着？它看起来省事得多——不用挑行权价、不用管到期日、像股票一样买入即可。 Given how expensive puts are and how relentlessly they must be rolled, a natural thought: why not just buy a long-volatility ETF and hold it? It looks far simpler — no strike to choose, no expiry to manage, buy it like a stock.

PRODUCT DOCUMENT · VXX

VXX 不是 VIX：先读它真正承诺跟踪什么VXX Is Not VIX: Read What It Actually Promises to Track

Cboe 的产品监管通知说得很明确：VXX 的回报链接至 S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures Index Total Return，该指数持有近月与次近月 VIX 期货，并逐日调整以维持约一个月的恒定到期暴露。它没有承诺按点对点复制现货 VIX，更没有承诺对你的股票组合进行固定赔付。 Cboe's product regulatory circular is explicit: VXX returns are linked to the S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures Index Total Return, which holds first- and second-month VIX futures and rebalances daily to maintain roughly one month of constant maturity. It does not promise point-for-point replication of spot VIX, and it does not promise a fixed payout against your equity portfolio.

当次近月期货高于近月（contango）时，这个逐日滚动会把暴露从较便宜的近月转向较贵的次近月，形成不利的展期项；backwardation 时方向可以反过来。正确的问题不是“VIX 会不会涨”，而是“这条期货指数路径与我要保护的损失路径是否匹配”。 When the second-month future is above the first (contango), the daily roll shifts exposure from the cheaper near contract toward the dearer next contract, creating an adverse roll component; in backwardation the direction can reverse. The right question is not "will VIX rise?" but "does this futures-index path match the loss path I want to insure?"

13.4 那么什么时候对冲是理性的13.4 So When Is Hedging Rational?

即使完整回测才能决定净成本，一个更重要的问题仍然成立：如果对冲可能降低收益，为什么还要付费？ Even though only a complete backtest can establish net cost, a more important question remains: if hedging may reduce return, why pay for it?

答案不仅在期望值，也在路径与约束里。第 9 章那条回撤曲线说过：−50% 需要 +100% 才能回本，而这个算术对一个必须在特定时间用钱的人尤其危险。如果你还有三十年才退休，可能有时间等待恢复；如果你明年要用这笔钱，同样的回撤可能迫使你在低点变现。对冲会改变回报分布，通常也会改变期望收益；它的价值要与负债期限、可承受回撤和成本一起评估。 The answer lies not only in expectancy but in path and constraints. Chapter 9's drawdown curve established that −50% requires +100% to recover, and that arithmetic is especially dangerous for someone who must spend the money on a particular date. Thirty years before retirement, there may be time to recover; if the money is needed next year, the same drawdown may force liquidation near a low. Hedging changes the return distribution and usually changes expected return too; its value must be evaluated alongside liability horizon, drawdown capacity, and cost.

按本章明示的 Black-Scholes 假设，每月滚动 5% 价外 Put 的毛保费支出会随 IV 大幅变化：平静、常态、紧张和恐慌四个示意情景分别为年化 4.5%、8.8%、19.3% 与 37.2%。这些不是历史策略回报，尚未扣除到期赔付、交易成本和路径影响。降低毛支出的常见结构手段包括买得更远、卖出保护腿形成价差，或只在预先定义的条件下部署；是否改善组合结果必须用完整净损益检验。VXX 也不能直接替代股票 Put：产品文件定义它跟踪逐日滚动的短期 VIX 期货指数，而不是现货 VIX 或你的股票损失。对冲的决定最终取决于负债期限、可承受回撤、成本和工具与风险的映射，不是媒体回报标题或单一波动率观点。 Under this chapter's disclosed Black–Scholes assumptions, the gross premium outlay for rolling a monthly 5%-OTM put changes sharply with IV: the calm, normal, tense and panic scenarios annualize to 4.5%, 8.8%, 19.3% and 37.2%. These are not historical strategy returns and do not include option payouts, trading costs or path effects. Common structural ways to lower gross outlay include buying farther out, selling a protective leg to form a spread, or deploying only under pre-defined conditions; whether any choice improves the portfolio must be tested on complete net P/L. VXX is not a direct substitute for an equity put either: its product documents define exposure to a daily-rolled short-term VIX futures index, not spot VIX or losses on your equities. The hedging decision ultimately depends on liability horizon, drawdown capacity, cost and the instrument-to-risk mapping—not a media return headline or a single volatility view.
