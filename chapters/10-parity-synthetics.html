Chapter 10 · Parity & Synthetics

平价关系与合成头寸：期权世界的守恒律Parity and Synthetics: The Conservation Law of Options

前九章的每一个结论，背后都站着一个模型和一堆假设。这一章不同。Put-Call Parity 是一个恒等式，不是一个模型——它不需要你相信波动率是常数、不需要正态分布、也不需要 Black-Scholes 是对的。它只需要"同样的东西不能有两个价格"。这是本书唯一一条不可能错的理论，而它的推论会重新排列你对前九章的理解。 Every conclusion in the first nine chapters rests on a model and a pile of assumptions. This chapter is different. Put-call parity is an identity, not a model — it doesn't ask you to believe volatility is constant, or that returns are lognormal, or that Black-Scholes is right. It asks only that the same thing cannot have two prices. It is the one piece of theory in this book that cannot be wrong, and its corollaries rearrange what you thought the first nine chapters said.

10.1 一个不需要模型的等式10.1 An Equation That Needs No Model

考虑两个组合。组合 A：买入一张 Call，同时卖出一张同 Strike、同到期日的 Put。组合 B：直接买入 100 股正股，并借入 K 的现值。 Consider two portfolios. Portfolio A: buy one call and sell one put at the same strike and expiry. Portfolio B: buy 100 shares outright and borrow the present value of K.

到期时，无论股价在哪里，这两个组合的价值完全相同。股价高于 K：A 的 Call 行权、Put 作废，你以 K 买入股票；B 你已经持股、还掉 K。股价低于 K：A 的 Put 被行权，你被迫以 K 买入股票；B 一样。两个组合在所有情形下都给出同一个结果，所以它们今天必须同价——否则任何人都能买低卖高、白拿差价。 At expiry, wherever the stock lands, the two portfolios are worth exactly the same. Above K: A's call is exercised and the put expires, so you buy at K; B already holds the shares and repays K. Below K: A's put is assigned and you are forced to buy at K; B, the same. Two portfolios that pay identically in every state must cost the same today — otherwise anyone could buy one, sell the other, and pocket the difference for free.

C − P = S − K · e−rT

这就是 Put-Call Parity（看涨看跌平价关系）。请注意它的右边没有波动率——没有 σ、没有 IV、没有任何关于"股价会怎么走"的假设。它只包含股价、行权价、利率和时间，全部是可观察的量。Black-Scholes 可能给出错误的价格，平价关系不会；前者是一个关于世界如何运转的猜测，后者只是一条会计恒等式。 This is put-call parity. Note what is absent from the right-hand side: no volatility, no IV, no assumption whatsoever about how the stock will move. It contains only spot, strike, rate and time — all observable. Black-Scholes can give you a wrong price; parity cannot. The former is a conjecture about how the world works; the latter is an accounting identity.

组合 A 的盈亏图值得亲眼看一次。买 Call + 卖同 Strike 的 Put，画出来是一条斜率为 1 的直线——和持有正股一模一样。期权的所有弯折都消失了。 Portfolio A's payoff is worth seeing once with your own eyes. Long call plus short put at the same strike draws a straight line of slope 1 — identical to holding the stock. Every kink an option has vanishes.

从这一章起，交互图上会多出一条橙色虚线。前九章的实线画的是到期那一刻的盈亏——所有时间价值都已归零；而虚线画的是此刻的盈亏，也就是把第 3 章讲的时间价值算进去之后，你的账户现在真实显示的数字。两条线之间的垂直距离，就是尚未流逝的时间价值。随着到期临近，虚线会一天天塌向实线——这正是 Theta，只不过现在你能亲眼看着它发生。灰色的竖点线标记开仓时的标的价格。 From this chapter on, the interactive charts carry an extra amber dashed line. The solid line, as in the first nine chapters, is P/L at expiry, when all time value has gone to zero. The dashed line is P/L right now — what your account actually shows once Chapter 3's time value is included. The vertical gap between them is the time value not yet surrendered. As expiry approaches, the dashed line collapses onto the solid one — that is theta, except now you can watch it happen. The faint vertical dotted line marks the underlying's price at entry.

这条等式的实际用途What the identity is actually for

你不会靠它套利——真实市场里这个价差早已被专业机构压到手续费以内。它的价值在于换算：任何一个头寸都可以用等价的另一组腿表达，而不同表达方式的手续费、保证金占用、税务处理和被指派风险完全不同。知道 A ≡ B，你就能在两者中挑成本更低的那个。本章接下来的一切都是这句话的展开。 You will not arbitrage with it — in real markets that spread is compressed inside transaction costs by professionals. Its value is translation: any position can be expressed as an equivalent set of legs, and different expressions carry different commissions, margin requirements, tax treatment and assignment risk. Knowing A ≡ B lets you pick the cheaper one. Everything that follows in this chapter unpacks that sentence.

这对第 2 章和第 7 章意味着什么What this does to Chapters 2 and 7

如果 Covered Call ≡ Short Put，那么第 7 章的 Wheel 就不是"两个策略的闭环"，而是同一个头寸在两种抵押形式之间来回切换：现金担保时叫 CSP，股票担保时叫 Covered Call。轮子转的不是策略，是抵押品。 If a covered call is a short put, then Chapter 7's Wheel isn't a loop between two strategies — it is one position alternating between two forms of collateral: cash-secured it's called a CSP, share-secured it's called a covered call. What the wheel turns is not the strategy; it's the collateral.

这不会让第 7 章的结论失效——恰恰相反，它解释了那个结论为什么成立。第 7 章说"Wheel 的盈亏在第 ① 步就决定了"，现在你知道原因：整个闭环从头到尾只有一个风险敞口，就是"在 Strike 附近持有这只股票"。转轮子换不掉它。而实操上真正的区别只有三个：资金占用、被指派后的税务事件、以及股票是否付股息。 This doesn't invalidate Chapter 7 — it explains why Chapter 7 was right. That chapter said the Wheel's P/L is settled at step ①; now you know the reason: the entire loop carries exactly one exposure, "own this stock around the strike," and turning the wheel never changes it. The genuine practical differences are only three: capital tied up, the taxable event on assignment, and whether the stock pays a dividend.

思考Think 既然 Covered Call 和 CSP 是同一个头寸，为什么绝大多数人觉得前者"稳健"、后者"激进"？如果两个风险完全相同的东西给人截然不同的感受，那么这种感受是在衡量风险，还是在衡量别的什么？把这个问题带回第 9 章的仓位规则：你的仓位上限，应该由头寸的形状决定，还是由它的名字决定？ If a covered call and a CSP are the same position, why do most people feel the first is "conservative" and the second "aggressive"? If two identically risky things produce opposite feelings, is that feeling measuring risk — or measuring something else? Carry the question back to Chapter 9's sizing rules: should your size limit be set by a position's shape, or by its name?

10.3 Box Spread：把平价关系做成一笔借贷10.3 The Box Spread: Parity Turned Into a Loan

把合成多头和合成空头放在两个不同的 Strike 上，你就得到一个 Box Spread（箱体价差）：在 K₁ 合成买入，在 K₂ 合成卖出。到期时，你以 K₁ 买、以 K₂ 卖，无论股价在哪里，收益恒等于 K₂ − K₁。 Put a synthetic long and a synthetic short at two different strikes and you have a box spread: synthetically buy at K₁, synthetically sell at K₂. At expiry you buy at K₁ and sell at K₂, so the payoff equals K₂ − K₁ regardless of where the stock is.

BOX SPREAD 100/110 四条腿，两条水平线four legs, two flat lines

这张图和本书其他任何一张都不一样：两条线都是水平的。股价从 $80 走到 $130，盈亏一动不动——四条腿把方向暴露完全抵消掉了。虚线（此刻）贴在零附近，实线（到期）停在约 +$0.10，两者之间那道窄缝就是三个月的利息。拖动"已过天数"滑块，你会看到虚线像债券计息一样，一天天匀速爬向实线——没有弯折，没有加速，因为这里根本没有时间价值可言，只有利息在累积。 This chart is unlike any other in the book: both lines are flat. The stock travels from $80 to $130 and the P/L does not move — four legs cancel the directional exposure completely. The dashed line (today) sits at zero, the solid one (at expiry) rests near +$0.10, and the narrow gap between them is three months of interest. Drag the days-elapsed slider and the dashed line climbs toward the solid one at a constant rate, exactly like a bond accruing — no kink, no acceleration, because there is no time value here at all, only interest adding up.

一个到期时必然值 $10、且与股价完全无关的东西——那不是期权头寸，那是一张零息债券。所以它今天的价格必然是 $10 的现值：10 · e−rT。按 4% 利率、三个月计，约 $9.90。 Something guaranteed to be worth $10 at expiry and utterly independent of the stock is not an options position — it is a zero-coupon bond. So today it must cost the present value of $10: 10 · e−rT, which at 4% over three months is about $9.90.

于是 Box Spread 有了一个非常实际的用途：买入 box = 放贷，卖出 box = 借款。机构用它在期权市场里以接近国债的利率融资，绕开券商的融资利率。卖出一个 box，你收到 $9.90，到期还 $10——这就是一笔利率 4% 的借款，只是穿着四条期权腿的外衣。 This gives the box a very practical use: buying one is lending, selling one is borrowing. Institutions use them to finance at near-Treasury rates inside the options market, bypassing their broker's margin rate. Sell a box, receive $9.90, repay $10 at expiry — that is a 4% loan wearing four option legs as a costume.

CASE FILE · 2019-01

"它字面意义上不可能爆仓""It Literally Cannot Go Tits Up"

2019 年 1 月，Reddit 论坛 r/wallstreetbets 上一位化名 1R0NYMAN 的用户发帖，宣布自己找到了一台印钞机。他在 Robinhood 上卖出 Box Spread 来"无风险"融资，并写下那句后来被反复引用的话："我没有任何资金处于风险之中"、"它字面意义上不可能爆仓"。 In January 2019 a user posting as 1R0NYMAN on Reddit's r/wallstreetbets announced he had found a money printer. He was selling box spreads on Robinhood to finance himself "risk-free," and wrote the line that would be quoted ever after: "I have no money at risk" and "it literally cannot go tits up."

他的推理并没有错——前提是那些期权是欧式的。box 的水平盈亏线只在"没有人能提前行权"时成立。而他交易的是 UVXY（一只波动率 ETF）的期权，美式条款：持有人可以在到期前的任何一天要求履约。 His reasoning was not wrong — provided the options were European. A box's flat payoff line holds only while nobody can exercise early. He was trading options on UVXY, a volatility ETF, under American-style terms: the holder may demand performance on any day before expiry.

UVXY 当时在 $50 附近，而他卖出的其中一条腿是 $15 的 Call——深度价内。对手方直接行权，他被迫按 $15 交出股票，box 的一条腿被拆走，剩下的三条腿瞬间从"零息债券"变成一个巨大的裸露方向头寸。 UVXY was trading near $50, and one of the legs he had sold was a $15 call — deep in the money. The counterparty simply exercised. He was forced to deliver at $15, one leg of the box was torn out, and the remaining three instantly stopped being a zero-coupon bond and became a large naked directional position.

10.4 平价关系管不到的地方10.4 Where Parity Has No Jurisdiction

平价关系强大，但它的管辖范围有明确边界，而理解这条边界正是第 3 章 Skew 的理论基础。 Parity is powerful, but its jurisdiction has a precise boundary — and understanding that boundary is the theoretical basis for Chapter 3's skew.

它管得到的：同一个 Strike 上的 Call 和 Put。由等式可以直接推出一个漂亮的结论——同一 Strike、同一到期日的 Call 和 Put，隐含波动率必然相等。因为它们的价格被恒等式锁死，而 Black-Scholes 是价格到 IV 的单调映射：价格锁死，IV 就锁死。如果你的行情软件显示同一 Strike 的 Call 和 Put 有不同的 IV，那不是市场机会，那是数据源用了不同的插值或时间戳。 Within jurisdiction: the call and put at one strike. A neat corollary follows straight from the identity — a call and a put at the same strike and expiry must have identical implied volatility. Their prices are pinned by the identity, and Black-Scholes maps price to IV monotonically: pin the price and you pin the IV. If your platform shows different IVs for a call and put at the same strike, that isn't an opportunity — it's a data feed using different interpolation or timestamps.

它管不到的：不同 Strike 之间。平价关系对 $90 的 Put 和 $110 的 Call 之间的相对价格一句话都没有说。市场因此完全自由地给它们定不同的 IV——而它确实这么做了，方向还非常固定：价外 Put 的 IV 系统性地高于价外 Call。第 8 章告诉过你这个偏斜从哪一天开始：1987 年 10 月 19 日。平价关系解释了为什么偏斜可以存在——因为没有任何恒等式禁止它。 Outside jurisdiction: across strikes. Parity says nothing whatsoever about the relative price of a $90 put and a $110 call. The market is therefore entirely free to assign them different IVs — and it does, in a very consistent direction: OTM puts carry systematically higher IV than OTM calls. Chapter 8 told you which day that began: October 19, 1987. Parity explains why skew is permitted to exist — because no identity forbids it.

一条可以直接用的推论A corollary you can use directly

既然同 Strike 的 Call 与 Put 由恒等式绑定，那么"买 Call" 和 "买股票 + 买 Put" 在风险上完全等价（第 2 章的 Protective Put 其实一直是一张合成 Call）。于是选择哪一个，就不再是风险判断，而是成本比较：哪个占用保证金少、哪个手续费低、哪个不会因为持股而在除息日被提前指派。把"我该用哪个策略"换成"同一个形状，哪种腿最便宜"，你的问题就从玄学变成了算术。 Since a call and put at one strike are bound by the identity, "buy a call" and "buy stock plus a put" are risk-equivalent — Chapter 2's protective put was a synthetic call all along. Choosing between them therefore isn't a risk judgment but a cost comparison: which ties up less margin, which costs less in fees, which avoids early assignment around an ex-dividend date. Replace "which strategy should I use" with "same shape — which set of legs is cheapest," and the question stops being metaphysics and becomes arithmetic.

10.5 Collar：为偏斜付账10.5 The Collar: Paying the Skew Bill

Collar（领口）在术语表里躺了九章，现在终于到了它的位置：持股 + 买入价外 Put + 卖出价外 Call，用卖 Call 的收入补贴买 Put 的保费。它常被宣传为"零成本对冲"，而它究竟能零成本到什么程度，完全由偏斜决定——这正是上一节那条边界的实际后果。 The collar has sat in the glossary for nine chapters; here is its place. Hold stock, buy an OTM put, sell an OTM call, and let the call premium subsidize the put. It is marketed as a "zero-cost hedge" — and exactly how zero-cost it can be is decided entirely by skew, which is the practical consequence of the boundary in §10.4.

Buying the −4.6% put and selling the +6.0% call does not net out: at illustrative IVs shaped like a typical SPX skew, those two legs cost roughly $44 per unit of index. 把偏斜的代价量化出来最能说明问题。用同一组示意 IV 反解"能让整个结构零成本的 Call 行权价"：在有偏斜的世界里，你只能卖出 +4.0% 的 Call；在假想的无偏斜世界（所有 Strike 同一 IV）里，你可以卖出 +7.2% 的 Call。同样的下跌保护，偏斜让你多交出 3.2 个百分点的上涨空间。而 JHEQX 实际的 +6.0% 落在这两条示意曲线之间——真实市场的偏斜确实在这个区间里。 Quantifying the skew bill makes the point best. Using the same illustrative IVs, solve for the call strike that makes the whole structure zero-cost: in a world with skew you can only sell the +4.0% call; in a hypothetical skew-free world (one IV at every strike) you could sell the +7.2% call. For identical downside protection, skew costs you 3.2 percentage points of upside. JHEQX's actual +6.0% sits between those two illustrative curves — real skew does live in that range.

保护的边界也要看清楚。指数跌到 −19.5% 之前，组合的损失被压在约 −4.6%；跌破 −19.5% 之后，卖出的那条 Put 开始反噬，保护失效。指数 −25% 时，带领口的组合约亏 −10.0%。这是一个为"大跌"设计、而非为"崩盘"设计的结构——第 4 章 ARKK 案例的同一个道理，只是这次的规模是 227 亿美元。 The boundary of the protection matters too. Down to −19.5%, the portfolio's loss is pinned near −4.6%; below −19.5% the short put bites back and the protection lapses. At −25% the collared portfolio loses about −10.0%. This is a structure built for a bad quarter, not for a crash — the same lesson as Chapter 4's ARKK case, at a scale of $22.7 billion.

Put-Call Parity 与 Black-Scholes 最根本的区别是什么？What is the fundamental difference between put-call parity and Black-Scholes?

C。等式右边 S − K·e−rT 里没有 σ——它不猜测未来，只要求"付款完全相同的两个组合必须同价"。D 正好说反了。B 有一点道理但不是"根本区别"：严格形式确实要求欧式（美式的提前行权会让等式变成不等式），而这正是 10.3 案例的要害。A 混淆了范畴：Black-Scholes 是模型，可能错；平价关系是恒等式，只要能借贷和做空就不可能错。 C. The right-hand side S − K·e−rT contains no σ — it forecasts nothing and requires only that two portfolios with identical payoffs cost the same. D says the opposite of the truth. 本章要点Key Takeaways

Put-Call Parity 是这本书里唯一不可能错的东西：C − P = S − K·e−rT 里没有波动率、没有分布假设，只有"付款相同的两个组合必须同价"。它的推论会重排你对前九章的理解——Covered Call 就是 Short Put（引擎逐点验证，差值恒为一个等于现金利息的常数 $1.09），于是第 7 章的 Wheel 不是两个策略的闭环，而是同一个头寸在现金抵押与股票抵押之间来回切换；Protective Put 一直是一张合成 Call，于是"用哪个策略"变成了"同一个形状，哪种腿最便宜"这道算术题。把四条腿凑成一个 Box，它连期权都不再是——那是一张零息债券，买入即放贷、卖出即借款，而 1R0NYMAN 用 $5,000 本金换来约 $58,000 欠款的全部原因，只是他用的期权带着"美式"两个字。这是本书第三次讲同一件事：伯克希尔靠欧式条款躲过 2009 年的 677 点，XIV 持有者被加速赎回条款剥夺了等待的资格，而这一次条款把无风险变成了债务。至于平价关系管不到的地方——不同 Strike 之间——市场就自由地开出了偏斜，而 JHEQX 每季度为它付账：同样的下跌保护，偏斜让这只 180 亿美元的基金多交出 3.2 个百分点的上涨空间。 Put-call parity is the one thing in this book that cannot be wrong: C − P = S − K·e−rT contains no volatility and no distributional assumption, only the requirement that two portfolios paying identically must cost identically. Its corollaries rearrange the first nine chapters. A covered call is a short put — verified point by point by the engine, differing by a constant $1.09 equal to the interest on the cash — so Chapter 7's Wheel is not a loop between two strategies but one position alternating between cash and share collateral. A protective put was a synthetic call all along, so "which strategy" becomes the arithmetic question "same shape, which legs are cheapest." Assemble four legs into a box and it stops being an options position at all: it is a zero-coupon bond, buying it is lending and selling it is borrowing — and the entire reason 1R0NYMAN turned $5,000 of capital into roughly $58,000 of debt is that his options carried the word "American." That is the third time this book has told one story: Berkshire's European terms erased March 2009's 677, XIV's acceleration clause denied its holders the right to wait, and here the fine print turned riskless into a debt. Where parity has no jurisdiction — across strikes — the market is free to charge skew, and JHEQX pays that bill every quarter: for identical downside protection, skew costs this $18 billion fund 3.2 percentage points of upside.
