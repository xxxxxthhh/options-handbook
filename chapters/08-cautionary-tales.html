Chapter 08 · Cautionary Tales

反面教材：裸卖、杠杆与波动率的墓碑Cautionary Tales: Naked Shorts, Leverage, and Volatility's Graveyard

这一章的三个案例有一个共同点，而且这个共同点会让你不舒服：他们中没有一个人是因为判断错误而死的。他们的模型是对的，历史回测是漂亮的，纪律是严格的，多数人还连续正确了好几年。他们死于同一件事——最坏情况没有下限，而他们的仓位是按"最坏情况不会发生"配置的。 The three cases in this chapter share something uncomfortable: not one of them died from being wrong. Their models were sound, their backtests handsome, their discipline real, and most of them were right for years running. They died of the same thing — a worst case with no floor, sized as though the worst case would not arrive.

8.1 为什么反面教材更值钱8.1 Why the Failures Teach More

前七章的案例大多在讲某个结构如何扩大利润或减少亏损。但那些案例有一个共同的取样偏差：它们都是幸存下来的故事。一个策略连续正确五年，然后在第六年归零，前五年的所有回测数据都会告诉你它很优秀——直到最后一个数据点出现。 The cases in the first seven chapters mostly show how a structure amplified profit or contained loss. They share a sampling bias: they are all stories that survived. A strategy that is right for five straight years and goes to zero in the sixth will show five years of excellent backtest data — right up until the final data point.

所以这一章的读法和前面不同。不要问"他们做错了什么"，要问"如果我在当时，我的做法和他们有什么实质区别"。如果答案是"我的仓位更小"，那你学到了这一章唯一要教的东西。如果答案是"我会看得更准"，那你还没有读懂。 So read this chapter differently. Don't ask "what did they do wrong," ask "had I been there, how would my approach have differed materially." If the answer is "my position would have been smaller," you've learned the one thing this chapter teaches. If the answer is "I'd have seen it coming," you haven't.

8.2 墓碑一：一夜蒸发 96% 的 XIV8.2 Headstone One: XIV, −96% Overnight

CASE FILE · 2018-02-05

Volmageddon：连续正确五年，然后用一个晚上还清Volmageddon: Five Years of Being Right, Repaid in One Night

XIV 是瑞士信贷发行的一只交易所交易票据，目标是提供 VIX 短期期货指数每日反向 1 倍的收益。翻译过来：它是一个打包好的、可以像股票一样买卖的做空波动率头寸。 XIV was an exchange-traded note issued by Credit Suisse, designed to deliver −1× the daily return of a short-term VIX futures index. Translated: a packaged short-volatility position you could buy and sell like a stock.

而它非常有效。VIX 期货长期处于contango（远月贵于近月），做空它就等于持续收取一笔结构性的展期收益。在第 5 章描述的那个史上最平静的 2017 年，XIV 上涨了约 188%。到 2018 年 1 月，它的规模已接近 19 亿美元，价格逼近 $145。五年的优异表现，把它从一个小众工具变成了散户账户里的常客。 And it worked extremely well. VIX futures sit in persistent contango (later months priced above nearer ones), so shorting them harvests a structural roll yield. Through the record-calm 2017 described in Chapter 5, XIV rose about 188%. By January 2018 it held close to $1.9 billion and traded near $145. Five years of excellence had turned a niche instrument into a fixture of retail accounts.

2018 年 2 月 5 日，标普 500 下跌 4.10%，VIX 单日上涨 115.6% 至 37.32——史上最大的单日涨幅。XIV 当日收盘 $99.00，但真正的清算发生在盘后：VIX 期货继续飙升，XIV 的指示价值在几小时内跌到约 $4.22，约 -96%。次日它收在约 $7.35。 On February 5, 2018 the S&P 500 fell 4.10% and the VIX rose 115.6% to 37.32 — its largest single-day gain ever. XIV closed the session at $99.00, but the real liquidation happened after hours: VIX futures kept surging and XIV's indicative value fell within hours to roughly $4.22, about −96%. It closed near $7.35 the following day.

这里出现了第 1 章那个教训的回声：条款。XIV 的招募文件里写着一条"加速赎回"条款——若单日指示价值下跌 80% 以上，发行方有权提前终止。瑞士信贷在 2 月 6 日行使了它。持有者甚至没有等待反弹的机会：这只票据于 2 月 21 日以 $5.99 清算，永久消失。波动率后来确实均值回归了，但那时已经没有 XIV 可以受益于此。 Here Chapter 1's lesson echoes: the fine print. XIV's prospectus contained an "acceleration" clause — if indicative value fell 80% or more in a day, the issuer could terminate early. Credit Suisse exercised it on February 6. Holders were not even given the chance to wait for a bounce: the note was liquidated at $5.99 on February 21 and ceased to exist. Volatility did mean-revert afterwards, but there was no longer any XIV to benefit from it.

8.3 墓碑二：亏损超过 100% 是可能的8.3 Headstone Two: Losing More Than 100% Is Possible

CASE FILE · 2018-11

裸卖天然气期权：客户不仅归零，还倒欠券商Naked Natural Gas: Clients Didn't Just Lose Everything, They Owed More

OptionSellers.com 是一家管理约 1.5 亿美元、服务约 290 位客户的资产管理公司。它的策略非常直白，甚至写在公司名字里：在商品市场上裸卖期权，收取权利金。这套做法运转了很多年。 OptionSellers.com managed roughly $150 million for about 290 clients. Its strategy was so plain it was in the company's name: sell naked options on commodities and collect the premium. It had worked for years.

2018 年 11 月，天然气价格失控。11 月 1 日前月合约约 $3.23；到 11 月 14 日，它冲到约 $4.84——两周约 +50%，其中 11 月 14 日单日盘中涨幅接近 18%。客户账户里是大量的裸卖看涨期权。 In November 2018 natural gas went vertical. The front-month contract was near $3.23 on November 1; by November 14 it reached about $4.84 — roughly +50% in two weeks, with nearly 18% of that intraday on November 14 alone. The client accounts were full of naked short calls.

NATURAL GAS · NOV 2018 （前月合约，$/MMBtu，约值）(front month, $/MMBtu, approx.)

结果不是"亏了很多"。结果是客户账户的亏损超过了账户里的全部资金——他们收到追加保证金通知，并且在清算之后倒欠清算券商一笔钱。创始人录制了一段流着泪的道歉视频，公司随后关闭。 The outcome wasn't "large losses." The losses exceeded the entire equity in the accounts — clients received margin calls and, after liquidation, owed money to the clearing broker. The founder recorded a tearful video apology and the firm shut down.

That isn't a flaw, it is this chapter's entire theme: a naked short's worst case is not in the picture, it's outside the picture — and your account balance is unaware of the distinction.

8.4 墓碑三：合成的保险不是保险8.4 Headstone Three: Synthetic Insurance Isn't Insurance

CASE FILE · 1987-10-19

黑色星期一：当保单需要你自己去执行Black Monday: When the Policy Requires You to Execute It

1987 年 10 月 19 日，道琼斯工业指数单日下跌 22.61%（508 点），标普 500 下跌 20.47%。这至今仍是美股历史上最大的单日跌幅，没有之一。 On October 19, 1987 the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 22.61% (508 points) and the S&P 500 fell 20.47%. It remains, to this day, the largest single-day decline in US market history.

DJIA · OCT 1987 （收盘点位）(closes)

当时市场上流行一种叫Portfolio Insurance（组合保险）的策略，据估计覆盖了数百亿美元的资产。它的想法在数学上完全正确：不去市场上买 Put，而是通过"随着市场下跌、动态卖出股指期货"来复制一份 Put 的收益曲线。理论上这能省下保费；毕竟 Put 的价格里包含着卖方的利润。 A strategy called portfolio insurance was then in vogue, covering tens of billions of dollars by most estimates. Its idea is mathematically correct: rather than buying puts, replicate a put's payoff by dynamically selling stock index futures as the market falls. In theory this saves the premium — after all, a put's price contains the seller's profit.

复制需要一个前提：你必须能够在下跌过程中持续地、按接近模型的价格卖出。而 10 月 19 日，这个前提消失了。所有采用该策略的机构在同一时刻收到同一个卖出信号，卖压压垮了流动性；期货相对现货出现巨大折价，模型要求的成交价根本不存在。更糟的是，这些机械的卖单本身加剧了下跌，从而触发了更多卖单。事后的布雷迪报告把组合保险认定为当日跌势的主要放大器之一。 Replication depends on one precondition: you must be able to keep selling, throughout the decline, at something close to model prices. On October 19 that precondition vanished. Every institution running the strategy received the same sell signal at the same moment, the selling overwhelmed liquidity, futures traded at enormous discounts to cash, and the prices the model assumed simply did not exist. Worse, the mechanical selling deepened the decline, which generated further sell signals. The subsequent Brady Commission report identified portfolio insurance as a principal amplifier of the day's crash.

这里是本章最深的一课，而且它直接回到第 1 章的定义。一份真正的 Put 是一份合约：对手方有法律义务履行，无论那天流动性如何、无论他有多不情愿。而一个复制策略只是你自己的交易能力——它在市场平静时与 Put 无异，在市场崩溃时一文不值。而这两种情形的区别，恰恰只在你需要它的那一天才显现。 Here is the chapter's deepest lesson, and it returns directly to Chapter 1's definitions. A real put is a contract: the counterparty is legally obliged to perform, whatever the liquidity that day and however little they'd like to. A replication strategy is merely your own ability to trade — indistinguishable from a put in calm markets, worthless in a collapse. And the difference between the two only ever reveals itself on the one day you need it.

Counterfactual：一位在 1987 年 10 月初买入真 Put 的投资者。1987 年之前指数期权几乎不存在波动率偏斜，价外 Put 相当便宜——按当时条件，一份三个月、行权价在现价下方 5% 的指数 Put，保费约为组合市值的 1.5%（示意值）。10 月 19 日之后：裸持仓单日 -20.47%；持有真 Put 的组合在 -5% 处止损，扣除约 1.5% 保费后约 -6.5%。而组合保险的使用者，得到的既不是 -6.5% 也不是 -20.47%，而是"模型说我应该被保护，但成交回报单说我没有"。 Counterfactual: an investor who bought real puts in early October 1987. Before 1987, index options carried almost no volatility skew and OTM puts were genuinely cheap — under the conditions of the time, a three-month index put struck 5% below spot cost roughly 1.5% of portfolio value (an illustrative figure). After October 19: unhedged, −20.47% on the day; with real puts, the loss floors at −5% and, net of the ~1.5% premium, lands near −6.5%. Portfolio-insurance users got neither −6.5% nor −20.47%, but rather "the model says I was hedged and the fills say I wasn't."

简化声明：指数跌幅、组合保险的规模量级与布雷迪报告的结论为真实历史。1.5% 的保费为示意值，用以说明"真 Put"与"合成 Put"在结果上的量级差异；1987 年的期权市场数据有限，此处不声称还原任何具体合约的价格。此外，一天之内的这场崩盘也会让真 Put 的持有者面临结算与对手方风险——只是当时的清算体系最终顶住了。 Stated simplification: the index declines, the order of magnitude of portfolio-insurance assets, and the Brady Commission's conclusion are real history. The 1.5% premium is illustrative, used to convey the magnitude of the gap between a real put and a synthetic one; 1987 options data is sparse and no specific contract price is claimed here. Note too that a one-day crash of this size also exposed real-put holders to settlement and counterparty risk — the clearing system merely happened to hold.

亏损减少：-20.47%（裸持仓）→ 约 -6.5%（真 Put，含约 1.5% 示意保费）；而合成复制在流动性消失的那一天根本无法执行Loss reduced: −20.47% unhedged → about −6.5% with real puts (including a ~1.5% illustrative premium) — while the synthetic replication simply could not be executed on the day liquidity vanished

1987 年留给今天的遗产What 1987 left behind

这一天永久改变了期权市场的形状。1987 年之前，指数期权在不同 Strike 上的 IV 大致相同；此后，价外 Put 的 IV 永久性地高于价外 Call——市场从此为下跌保险索取额外的价格。这就是第 3 章提到的波动率偏斜，它不是某个数学性质，而是一整代交易员对同一天的记忆被刻进了价格里。你今天买一份 SPY 保护性 Put 时多付的那部分钱，付的就是 1987 年 10 月 19 日。 That day permanently changed the shape of the options market. Before 1987, index option IV was roughly uniform across strikes; afterwards, OTM puts have permanently carried higher IV than OTM calls — the market has charged extra for downside insurance ever since. This is Chapter 3's volatility skew, and it is not a mathematical property but an entire generation of traders' memory of a single day, encoded into prices. The extra you pay for a protective SPY put today is a payment toward October 19, 1987.

8.5 三块墓碑上的同一句话8.5 The Same Sentence on All Three Headstones

把三个案例并排，共同点清晰得刺眼。它们都不是判断错误。XIV 的持有者相信波动率会均值回归——他们是对的，波动率确实回归了，只是 XIV 已经被清算。裸卖天然气的人相信气价会留在区间内——多年来他们都是对的。组合保险的使用者相信可以用动态对冲复制 Put——在数学上他们完全正确。 Set the three side by side and the commonality is glaring. None of them was a failure of judgment. XIV's holders believed volatility mean-reverts — they were right, it did, only XIV had already been liquidated. The naked gas sellers believed prices would stay in range — for years they were right. Portfolio insurance's users believed a put could be replicated by dynamic hedging — mathematically they were entirely correct.

第 ② 条最值得停下来想。连续的成功不是风险的减少，它是仓位的放大器。2017 年 XIV 涨了 188%，这个数字本身就在说服每一个持有者加仓；OptionSellers 多年的平稳收益，正是它能募集到 1.5 亿美元的原因。换句话说，这些策略的崩溃规模，恰恰与它们此前的成功程度成正比。这不是巧合，是结构。 The second deserves a pause. A run of success isn't a reduction in risk, it's an amplifier of size. XIV's 188% in 2017 was itself an argument to every holder for adding more; OptionSellers' years of smooth returns are exactly why it could raise $150 million. Put differently: the scale of these blow-ups was proportional to how successful they had previously been. That's not a coincidence, it's the structure.

"这次不一样"的正确用法The correct use of "this time is different"

读完这一章，很容易得出"永远不要做卖方"的结论。那是过度纠正，而且是错的。本手册第 2 章的 Covered Call 和 CSP 都是卖方策略，第 4 章的贷记价差、第 5 章的 Iron Condor 也是——它们全都有抵押或有保护腿。这一章反对的从来不是"卖方"，而是三个具体的东西：没有抵押、没有上限、没有仓位纪律。区分它们的方法只有一个问题，而且这个问题在开仓前一分钟就能回答："如果标的明天跳空 30%，我会亏多少？"如果你答不出一个具体的数字，你就不该开这个仓。 It's easy to finish this chapter concluding "never be a seller." That's an overcorrection, and it's wrong. Chapter 2's covered calls and CSPs are selling strategies, as are Chapter 4's credit spreads and Chapter 5's iron condors — every one of them collateralized or winged. This chapter never argued against selling; it argued against three specific things: no collateral, no ceiling, no size discipline. One question separates them, and it can be answered a minute before you enter: "if the underlying gaps 30% tomorrow, how much do I lose?" If you can't produce a specific number, don't open the position.

思考Think 2017 年底，一位持有 XIV 的投资者已经连续正确了五年，账户翻了数倍。在那个时点，有什么证据能说服他减仓？他的回测是漂亮的，他的逻辑是对的，市场每天都在奖励他。如果"过往表现"和"逻辑正确"都无法提供减仓的理由——那么仓位的上限究竟应该由什么决定？第 9 章会正面回答这个问题。 At the end of 2017, an XIV holder had been right for five straight years and multiplied their account. At that moment, what evidence could have persuaded them to cut? The backtest was beautiful, the logic was sound, the market rewarded them daily. If neither past performance nor correct reasoning can supply a reason to reduce — then what should determine the size limit at all? Chapter 9 answers that directly.

1987 年组合保险失效的根本原因是什么？What fundamentally caused portfolio insurance to fail in 1987?

A real put is a contract whose counterparty is legally bound; a replication is merely your own ability to trade — and the difference only appears on the day you need it most.

本章要点Key Takeaways

三块墓碑上刻着同一句话，而它不是"他们判断错了"。XIV 的持有者对波动率均值回归的判断是对的，只是"加速赎回"条款让他们没能活到回归发生；裸卖天然气的人对价格区间的判断多年都是对的，只是 2018 年 11 月的那一次让亏损穿透了账户底部、变成了对券商的欠款；组合保险在数学上完全正确，只是它依赖一个在崩盘日必然失效的前提——你能持续以模型价格成交。把它们拼在一起，得到的是一条比任何策略都重要的规律：连续的成功不是风险变小的证据，它是仓位的放大器，所以崩溃的规模总是与此前的成功程度成正比。这一章反对的从来不是做卖方——本手册一半的策略都是卖方——它反对的是三件具体的事：没有抵押、没有上限、没有仓位纪律。而区分它们只需要一个能在下单前一分钟回答的问题：如果明天跳空 30%，我亏多少？答不出具体数字，就不要开这个仓。 All three headstones carry the same sentence, and it isn't "they were wrong." XIV's holders were right that volatility mean-reverts; an acceleration clause simply ensured they weren't around when it did. The naked gas sellers were right about the price range for years; November 2018 merely pushed the loss through the floor of the account and turned it into a debt to the broker. Portfolio insurance was mathematically correct; it just rested on a precondition guaranteed to fail on a crash day — that you can keep transacting at model prices. Assembled, they yield a rule more important than any strategy: a run of success is not evidence that risk has fallen, it is an amplifier of size — which is why the scale of a blow-up is always proportional to the success that preceded it. This chapter never argued against selling; half the strategies in this handbook are selling strategies. It argues against three specific things: no collateral, no ceiling, no size discipline. And one question, answerable a minute before you enter, separates them: if it gaps 30% tomorrow, how much do I lose? No specific number means no position.
