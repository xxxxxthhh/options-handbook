Chapter 07 · The Wheel

Wheel 系统：CSP → 指派 → Covered Call 的闭环The Wheel: CSP → Assignment → Covered Call

把第 2 章的两个卖方策略首尾相接，就得到了一套可以无限循环的系统。它的宣传语是"用纪律换取持续现金流"，这没错。但它真正的性质是另一句话：Wheel 不是一台印钞机，它是一套让你必须提前说清楚"我愿意在什么价格买、在什么价格卖"的强制程序。 Join Chapter 2's two selling strategies end to end and you get a system that can run indefinitely. Its pitch — "discipline converted into steady cash flow" — is fair. But its real nature is a different sentence: the Wheel isn't a money printer, it's a procedure that forces you to state, in advance, the price at which you'd buy and the price at which you'd sell.

7.1 闭环7.1 The Loop

① 卖 CSP① Sell a CSP
收租，等着接货Collect rent, wait to be filled
② 被指派② Get assigned
按 Strike 买入 100 股Buy 100 shares at the strike
③ 卖 Covered Call③ Sell a covered call
继续收租Keep collecting rent
④ 被 call 走④ Get called away
卖出股票，回到 ①Shares sold, back to ①

这个循环的优雅之处在于，每一步都是你在第 2 章已经学会的东西，而且每一步都产生现金。没有指派时，你收 CSP 的权利金；被指派后，你持有想要的股票并收 Covered Call 的权利金；被 call 走时，你按预设价格卖出并回到起点。看上去每一个分支都有利可图。 The loop's elegance is that every step is something Chapter 2 already taught you, and every step produces cash. Unassigned, you keep the CSP premium; assigned, you own a stock you wanted and collect covered call premium; called away, you sell at your pre-set price and return to the start. Every branch appears to pay.

这正是危险所在。"每个分支都有利可图"这个表象，掩盖了一个事实：所有分支的盈亏，都取决于同一个在第 ① 步就已经做出的决定——你选了哪只股票、定在哪个 Strike。Wheel 的管理技巧再高明，也无法修正一个错误的第 ① 步。本章剩下的内容，基本都在说明这一句话。 Which is exactly the danger. "Every branch pays" conceals the fact that every branch's P/L depends on a single decision already made at step ① — which stock you chose, and at what strike. No amount of skilled management repairs a wrong step ①. Most of this chapter exists to demonstrate that sentence.

7.2 第 ① 步：CSP 的三个前提7.2 Step ①: The CSP's Three Preconditions

第 2 章已经给过一个前提："Strike 是你真心愿意持有这家公司的价格。"运转一整套系统需要把它拆成三条，而且三条必须同时成立： Chapter 2 already gave one: "the strike must be a price at which you genuinely want to own the business." Running a full system requires splitting that into three, and all three must hold simultaneously:

① 标的① The name
被套牢三年也不后悔You'd hold it for three years
② 价格② The price
Strike 是你想买的价位The strike is a price you want
③ 仓位③ The size
全部被指派也不伤筋动骨Full assignment is survivable

注意这三条里没有一条提到权利金。这不是疏忽。Wheel 最常见的死法，是把顺序倒过来：先扫描哪只股票的权利金年化最高，再回过头去说服自己"这家公司其实也还行"。而权利金最肥的地方，恰恰是市场认为风险最大的地方——IV 高不是免费的午餐，它是市场对真实风险的报价。 Note that none of the three mentions premium. That is not an oversight. The Wheel's commonest cause of death is running the list backwards: first screen for the highest annualized premium, then talk yourself into "the company's actually fine." And the fattest premium sits exactly where the market sees the most risk — high IV is not a free lunch, it is the market's quote for real danger.

7.3 第 ② 步：被指派不是失败7.3 Step ②: Assignment Is Not a Failure

新手最容易在这一步产生情绪。收到指派通知的那一刻，账户里突然出现 100 股正股和一笔浮亏，感觉像是"出事了"。但按照设计，指派是这套系统的正常工作状态，不是异常——你从一开始收的钱，买的就是"在 Strike 接货"这个义务。 This is where beginners get emotional. The assignment notice arrives, 100 shares and an unrealized loss appear in the account, and it feels like something broke. By design, assignment is this system's normal operating state, not an exception — the obligation to take delivery at the strike is precisely what you were paid for from the outset.

唯一需要立刻做对的事，是把成本基准算清楚。你的成本不是 Strike，而是 Strike 减去你收到的所有权利金。这个数字将决定后面每一步 Covered Call 的 Strike 该定在哪里，也是判断"这笔交易到底是赚是亏"的唯一标尺。 The one thing to get right immediately is your cost basis. Your cost is not the strike; it is the strike minus every premium you've collected. That number determines where each subsequent covered call's strike belongs, and it is the only yardstick for whether the trade is actually up or down.

CASE FILE · 2020

崩盘中被指派的微软：Wheel 最好的一天，看起来像最坏的一天Assigned Microsoft in the Crash: The Wheel's Best Day Looks Like Its Worst

2020 年 2 月 21 日，微软收于约 $178.59，疫情尚未反映到价格里。两个人同时看多微软，做法不同： On February 21, 2020, Microsoft closed near $178.59, the pandemic not yet in the price. Two people were bullish on Microsoft and acted differently:

A
直接买入 100 股 @ $178.59Buys 100 shares at $178.59
B
卖出 $170 CSP，收 $4.00Sells the $170 CSP for $4.00

接下来五周是史上最快的熊市。3 月 20 日微软跌到约 $137，B 被指派——他按 $170 买入了一只当时市价 $137 的股票，账面立刻浮亏约 19%。在那个星期五，B 看起来像是做了一笔糟糕透顶的交易。但他的成本基准是 $170 − $4 = $166，而 A 的成本是 $178.59。 The next five weeks were the fastest bear market on record. By March 20 Microsoft was near $137 and B was assigned — buying at $170 a stock then quoted at $137, an immediate paper loss of about 19%. That Friday, B looked like someone who had made a catastrophic trade. But B's cost basis was $170 − $4 = $166, against A's $178.59.

MSFT · 2020 （收盘价与 B 的成本基准）(closes, with B's cost basis)

到 2020 年 12 月 31 日，微软收于 $222.42。A 的回报是 (222.42 − 178.59) ÷ 178.59 = +24.5%；B 按投入的 $17,000 现金计算，回报是 (222.42 − 170 + 4) ÷ 170 = +33.2%。同一个正确的判断，B 多赚了将近九个百分点，全部来自"他没有在 2 月买，而是让市场在 3 月把股票送到他的价位"。 By December 31, 2020, Microsoft closed at $222.42. A returned (222.42 − 178.59) ÷ 178.59 = +24.5%; B, against the $17,000 of cash committed, returned (222.42 − 170 + 4) ÷ 170 = +33.2%. The same correct view, and B earned nearly nine points more — entirely because B didn't buy in February, but let the market deliver the shares at B's price in March.

简化声明：微软股价为真实历史收盘约值。$4.00 的权利金是按当时约 30 天、约 5% 价外的 Put 给出的示意值，非实际成交价。计算按现金全额担保口径，忽略手续费与被指派时点的精确差异；也未计入 B 在指派后本应继续卖出的 Covered Call。 Stated simplification: Microsoft's prices are approximate real closes. The $4.00 premium is an illustrative value for a roughly 30-day, roughly 5%-OTM put, not an actual fill. The math assumes full cash securing and ignores commissions and the exact assignment timing; it also excludes the covered calls B would have gone on to sell.

利润扩大：同一个看多微软的判断，2 月直接买入 +24.5% → CSP 在崩盘中接货 +33.2%Profit amplified: the same bullish view, +24.5% buying outright in February → +33.2% taking delivery via the CSP in the crash

但闭环的下半程会削掉这个优势But the loop's second half erodes that edge

上面的计算刻意省略了一件事：按 Wheel 的规则，B 在 3 月被指派后应该开始卖 Covered Call。而 2020 年 4 月到 12 月是一段几乎笔直向上的 V 形反弹——微软从 $137 涨到 $222。在这样的行情里，任何一个价外 Call 都会在几周内被击穿，股票被 call 走，B 会在 $180、$190 的位置反复踏空后面的涨幅。换句话说，帮 B 赚到 +33.2% 的是 CSP 那一半，而 Covered Call 那一半会把它还回去不少。这正是第 2 章那个结论的又一次现身：Covered Call 在单边上涨中"赚小钱、丢大钱"，而 Wheel 内置了这个缺陷。 The calculation above deliberately omits something: by the Wheel's own rules, B should have started selling covered calls after the March assignment. And April through December 2020 was a nearly straight-line V-shaped recovery, Microsoft running from $137 to $222. In that tape any OTM call gets breached within weeks, the shares are called away, and B repeatedly steps off the escalator at $180, at $190. Put plainly: the +33.2% came from the CSP half, and the covered-call half would have given a good deal of it back. This is Chapter 2's conclusion resurfacing: covered calls earn small money and forfeit big money in a one-way rally, and the Wheel has that flaw built in.

7.4 第 ③ 步：Covered Call 的 Strike 只有一条规则7.4 Step ③: The Covered Call's Strike Has One Rule

被指派后开始卖 Call，Strike 的选择只有一条硬规则：不要低于你的成本基准。把 Strike 定在成本基准之下，意味着如果被 call 走，你锁定了一笔亏损——你用一点点权利金，买断了自己回本的可能性。 Once assigned, selling calls comes with one hard rule for the strike: never below your cost basis. A strike beneath your basis means that being called away locks in a loss — you sold your own chance of getting back to even for a sliver of premium.

STEP ③ · COVERED CALL Strike 高于成本基准时的形状the shape when the strike sits above your basis

这条规则听起来是常识，直到你遇到这样的情形：股价已经跌到成本基准的一半，而成本基准之上的 Call 几乎不值钱——卖一个月只能收到几分钱，等于没有收入。这时候 Wheel 的闭环就卡住了：你不能卖低于成本的 Call（会锁定亏损），也没法从高于成本的 Call 收到有意义的租金。系统停转，你只剩下一个被套的正股头寸和一堆无处安放的纪律。 That sounds like common sense until you meet this situation: the stock has halved relative to your basis, and calls above the basis are nearly worthless — a month's premium is a few cents, which is to say no income at all. At that point the loop jams: you can't sell calls below your basis (that locks in the loss), and calls above it pay no meaningful rent. The system stops turning, leaving you an underwater share position and a set of rules with nothing to do.

7.5 Wheel 唯一的真实风险：你选了什么7.5 The Wheel's Only Real Risk: What You Picked

CASE FILE · 2022

在英特尔上跑 Wheel：租金收得很勤，刀也接得很稳Running the Wheel on Intel: Diligent Rent, Steady Knife

2022 年初，英特尔约 $51.5，股息率超过 3%，市盈率个位数，IV 又足够高到让卖 Put 的年化收益很好看。它在每一个"Wheel 选股清单"上都会被打上高分。到 2022 年 12 月 30 日，它收在 $26.43——全年约 -48.7%。 In early 2022, Intel traded near $51.5, with a dividend yield above 3%, a single-digit P/E, and IV high enough to make put-selling yields look excellent. It would have scored well on every "Wheel screening checklist" in existence. By December 30, 2022 it closed at $26.43 — roughly −48.7% on the year.

一位纪律严明的 Wheel 交易者会这样走完这一年：1 月卖出 $48 的 CSP 收 $1.80；2 月被指派，成本基准 $46.20。此后按 7.4 的规则，Covered Call 的 Strike 不得低于 $46.20——3 至 5 月股价还在 $44–50，这样的 Call 每月还能收约 $0.5–0.7；6 月起股价跌破 $40，$46.20 之上的 Call 每月只值一毛钱上下；9 月以后接近于零。全年租金合计约 $3.90，而不是"每月稳定收租"所想象的 $7–8。 A disciplined Wheel trader would have walked the year like this: sell the $48 CSP in January for $1.80; be assigned in February at a $46.20 cost basis. From there, §7.4's rule pins every covered-call strike at $46.20 or above — through March–May, with the stock at $44–50, such calls still paid about $0.5–0.7 a month; from June, with the stock under $40, calls above $46.20 were worth a dime or so; from September, next to nothing. Total rent for the year: about $3.90 — not the $7–8 that "steady monthly rent" imagines.

INTC · 2022 （收盘价与成本基准 $46.20）(closes vs. the $46.20 cost basis)

年末结算：(26.43 − 48 + 3.90) ÷ 48 = 约 -37%，而直接买入并持有的人是 -48.7%。从数字上看，Wheel 仍然赢了约十二个百分点。如果案例到此为止，它会是一个 Wheel 的成功故事。 Year-end: (26.43 − 48 + 3.90) ÷ 48 = about −37%, against −48.7% for someone who simply bought and held. On the numbers, the Wheel still won by about twelve points. If the case stopped here it would read as a Wheel success story.

但请看图中那条平线。卡死不是发生在某一天，而是一段渐进的窒息：6 月股价跌到 $37.4 时，$46.20 之上的 Call 已经只值一毛钱，租金名存实亡；到 9 月股价 $25.77，它彻底归零。这时唯一还能收到像样租金的，是 $27.75 附近（5% 价外）的 Call——卖出它，就是同意以 $27.75 交出成本 $46.20 的股票，一次性锁定每股约 $18.45 的亏损。于是唯一"合规"的选择是停止卖 Call——而停止卖 Call 意味着这套系统不再产生任何现金流。Wheel 的下半程从 6 月起名存实亡，9 月起彻底不存在，剩下的只是一个被套的正股头寸。 But look at the flat line on the chart. The jam wasn't a single day; it was a gradual suffocation: by June, with the stock at $37.4, calls above $46.20 were worth a dime and the rent existed in name only; by September, at $25.77, it was zero. The only call still paying meaningful rent sat near $27.75 (5% OTM) — and selling it means agreeing to hand over $46.20-basis shares at $27.75, crystallizing about $18.45 per share of loss in one stroke. So the only "compliant" choice is to stop selling calls — and stopping means the system produces no cash flow at all. From June the Wheel's second half existed in name only; from September, not at all. What remained was an underwater stock position.

这就是本章的核心结论。租金不是护城河，它是缓冲垫。$3.90 的租金把 -48.7% 垫到了约 -37%，这是真实的减损；但它没有、也不可能修正第 ① 步的判断——2022 年的英特尔不是一只"跌下来就该买"的股票，而是一家基本面正在恶化的公司。Wheel 把"我愿意在 $48 买入英特尔"这个决定货币化了，可它无法让这个决定变得正确。 And that is this chapter's central conclusion. Rent is not a moat, it is a cushion. The $3.90 padded −48.7% into about −37%, a genuine reduction; but it did not and could not correct the judgment made at step ① — 2022 Intel was not a stock to buy on weakness, it was a company whose fundamentals were deteriorating. The Wheel monetized the decision "I'd buy Intel at $48." It could not make that decision right.

简化声明：英特尔股价为真实历史收盘约值。CSP 与 Covered Call 的权利金（$1.80，以及按各月股价对 $46.20 之上 Strike 估算的 $0.05–0.7/月）为按当时 IV 水平给出的示意值，非实际成交价；计算忽略手续费、股息、以及实盘中几乎必然发生的 Roll 操作。 Stated simplification: Intel's prices are approximate real closes. The CSP and covered-call premiums ($1.80, plus $0.05–0.7 a month estimated against strikes above $46.20 at each month's stock price) are illustrative values at the era's IV levels, not actual fills; the math ignores commissions, dividends, and the rolls that would almost certainly have occurred in a live account.

亏损减少：-48.7%（裸持有）→ 约 -37%（Wheel 收租），但 7.4 的规则让租金从 6 月起枯竭——缓冲垫恰好在最需要的时候变薄Loss reduced: −48.7% buy-and-hold → about −37% with Wheel rent — but §7.4's rule dried the rent up from June, thinning the cushion exactly when it was needed most

思考Think 微软的案例和英特尔的案例，操作步骤完全相同：卖 CSP、被指派、算成本基准、卖 Covered Call。两者的结果相差七十个百分点。如果流程一样而结果天差地别，那么这套"系统"到底在系统化什么？它系统化的是执行，还是判断？如果只是执行，那么把它称作"策略"是否夸大了它的作用？ The Microsoft case and the Intel case follow identical steps: sell the CSP, get assigned, compute the basis, sell covered calls. Their outcomes differ by seventy percentage points. If the process is the same and the results are not, what is this "system" actually systematizing? Execution, or judgment? And if only execution, does calling it a "strategy" overstate what it does?

7.6 状态过滤与"不交易"7.6 Regime Filters and the "No Trade" Output

Wheel 是一个做多 + 做空波动率的组合头寸。把它翻译成第 3 章的语言：净 Delta 为正，净 Vega 为负，净 Theta 为正，净 Gamma 为负。这四个符号已经把它的适用环境写死了——它需要一个不涨太快、也不跌太狠的市场。 The Wheel is a combined long delta plus short volatility position. In Chapter 3's language: net delta positive, net vega negative, net theta positive, net gamma negative. Those four signs already fix its habitat — it needs a market that neither rises too fast nor falls too hard.

于是本手册的主线在这里第一次成为一条可执行的规则：先判断 Regime，再决定要不要转动轮子。当你手上的标的正处在一个明确的下降趋势里，最正确的操作不是把 Strike 定得更低、把 DTE 拉得更短、或者把权利金收得更勤——而是不开这个仓。 So the book's through-line becomes an executable rule here for the first time: read the regime before deciding whether to turn the wheel. When your candidate sits in a clear downtrend, the correct action is not a lower strike, a shorter DTE, or more frequent premium collection — it is not opening the position at all.

"不交易"是这套系统的合法输出"No trade" is a legitimate output of this system

Wheel 有一个心理陷阱：它是一套循环系统，而循环系统天然给人"必须一直转"的暗示。空仓的一个月会让人觉得自己在浪费资金效率，于是去找一个次优的标的把轮子转起来。但这套系统的期望收益，几乎全部来自那些你正确地拒绝了的交易。2022 年在英特尔上严格按规则执行的人，输给了看一眼趋势然后什么都没做的人——整整三十七个百分点。把"本月无合格标的"写进你的规则里，让它和"卖出 CSP"享有同等地位。 The Wheel carries a psychological trap: it is a cyclical system, and cyclical systems imply they must always be turning. A month in cash feels like wasted capital efficiency, so people find a second-best name just to keep the wheel moving. But this system's expected return comes almost entirely from the trades you correctly declined. Someone who ran Intel by the rules in 2022 lost to someone who glanced at the trend and did nothing — by a full thirty-seven points. Write "no qualifying candidate this month" into your rules, and give it equal standing with "sell the CSP."

B。你按 $50 买入，但此前已收到 $2，实际付出 $48。这个数字有实际用途：它是后面所有 Covered Call 的 Strike 下限。D 是常见误解——指派时的市价决定你的浮亏，但不决定你的成本，两者是不同的东西。 B. You bought at $50 having already collected $2, so you laid out $48. 扫描期权链时你发现某只股票的 CSP 年化收益率高达 60%。这最可能意味着什么？Screening chains, you find a stock whose CSP shows a 60% annualized yield. This most likely means:

D。权利金高等价于 IV 高，而 IV 是市场对波动幅度的报价，不是慈善。60% 的年化在说：市场认为这只股票很可能在到期前大幅下跌。B 恰恰把 Wheel 的选股顺序做反了——先看收益率再找理由，是这套系统最常见的死法。A 假设自己比整个市场更聪明，这需要证据而不是一个筛选器。 D. Rich premium means high IV, and IV is the market's quote for magnitude, not charity. A 60% annualized yield is saying the market thinks this stock may well fall hard before expiry. B runs the Wheel's selection order exactly backwards — yield first, rationale afterwards, which is this system's most common cause of death. A assumes you're smarter than the whole market, which requires evidence rather than a screener.

你的成本基准是 $46，股价已跌到 $26。一个 $28 的 Call 每月能收 $0.60。你应该卖吗？Your cost basis is $46 and the stock is at $26. A $28 call pays $0.60 a month. Should you sell it?

C。这就是英特尔案例里闭环卡死的那一刻。$0.60 的租金看起来是白捡的，但它的代价是：一旦股价反弹到 $28 以上，你必须以 $28 交出成本 $46 的股票——用 $0.60 买断了 $18 的回本可能。B 尤其危险，它把"锁定亏损"说成了"加快回本"。D 是自欺：低 Delta 意味着概率低，不意味着不会发生，而这里"发生"的代价是灾难性的。 C. This is the exact moment the Intel loop jammed. The $0.60 looks free, but its price is this: if the stock recovers past $28 you must deliver $46-basis shares at $28 — selling away $18 of recovery for $0.60. B is especially dangerous, dressing "locking in a loss" as "speeding up recovery." D is self-deception: low delta means low probability, not impossibility, and here the consequence of "possible" is catastrophic.

Wheel 在哪种市场状态下表现最差？In which regime does the Wheel perform worst?

A。Wheel 是"净 Delta 为正 + 净 Vega 为负"的组合，所以两端都吃亏：急涨时它被 call 走反复踏空（微软 2020 年反弹段的问题），趋势熊时它接刀且闭环卡死（英特尔 2022）。它需要的是中间地带——震荡或缓涨，所以 D 恰恰是它最好的环境。B、C 各只说对了一半。 A. The Wheel is net long delta and net short volatility, so it's hurt at both ends: in a melt-up it's called away and keeps missing the move (Microsoft's 2020 recovery), and in a trending bear it catches knives until the loop jams (Intel 2022). It needs the middle ground — chop or a slow grind up — which makes D its best environment, not its worst. B and C are each half right.

这个月你扫描了所有候选标的，没有一只同时满足"愿意长期持有"和"Strike 是理想买价"。最好的做法是？This month nothing on your list satisfies both "I'd hold it long term" and "the strike is a price I want." The best action is:

B。A、C、D 有一个共同点：它们都在调整参数，而问题出在前提。更低的 Strike、更短的 DTE、更小的仓位，都不能把一只你并不想持有的股票变成合格标的——它们只是让同一个错误变得更便宜一点。"本月无合格标的"是这套系统的正常输出，不是失败。这套系统的期望收益，很大一部分来自你正确拒绝的那些交易。 B. A, C and D share a defect: they adjust parameters when the problem is a precondition. A lower strike, a shorter DTE, a smaller size — none converts a stock you don't want into a qualifying candidate; they only make the same mistake cheaper. "No qualifying candidate this month" is a normal output of this system, not a failure. A large share of the system's expected return comes from the trades you correctly declined.

本章要点Key Takeaways

Wheel 把第 2 章的两个卖方策略接成闭环，每一步都产生现金，因而很容易被误认为是一台印钞机。但微软和英特尔这两个案例走的是完全相同的四个步骤，结果却相差七十个百分点——这说明这套系统系统化的是执行，而不是判断。它的全部盈亏，在第 ① 步选定标的与 Strike 的那一刻就已经基本决定：微软在 2020 年 3 月被指派是这套系统最好的一天（成本基准 $166，最终 +33.2%），英特尔在 2022 年被指派则让租金从六月起枯竭、闭环彻底卡死，留下一张最大收益为负的盈亏图。租金不是护城河，它是缓冲垫——$3.90 的租金能把 -48.7% 垫到约 -37%，但它无法把一个错误的买入决定变成正确的。所以这一章真正的技术含量不在管理，而在过滤：先看 Regime，再看标的，最后才看权利金；而当三个前提无法同时成立时，"本月不开仓"和"卖出 CSP"是这套系统同等合法的两个输出。 The Wheel joins Chapter 2's two selling strategies into a loop where every step produces cash, which makes it easy to mistake for a money printer. But the Microsoft and Intel cases run exactly the same four steps to outcomes seventy points apart — which tells you the system systematizes execution, not judgment. Its entire P/L is largely settled the moment step ① picks the name and the strike: being assigned Microsoft in March 2020 was this system's finest day ($166 basis, +33.2% eventually), while being assigned Intel in 2022 dried the rent up from June, jammed the loop completely, and left a payoff diagram whose maximum gain is negative. Rent is not a moat, it is a cushion — $3.90 of it padded −48.7% into about −37%, but it could not turn a wrong purchase decision into a right one. So this chapter's real skill lies in filtering rather than managing: read the regime, then the name, and only then the premium; and when the three preconditions can't hold together, "nothing this month" and "sell the CSP" are equally legitimate outputs of the system.
