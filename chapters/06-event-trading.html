Chapter 06 · Event Trading

事件交易：财报、IV Crush 与价差的用法Event Trading: Earnings, IV Crush, and Spread Design

财报是散户最爱的期权场景，也是亏钱最快的场景。原因不是他们看错了公司，而是他们没有意识到：在事件交易里，你面对的从来不是"公司会怎样"，而是"公司会不会比市场已经定好的预期更极端"。这一章把这个差距拆到分毫。 Earnings is retail's favorite options setting and the fastest place to lose money. Not because people read the company wrong, but because they miss this: in event trading you never face "what will the company do," you face "will the company be more extreme than the expectation already priced." This chapter takes that gap apart to the cent.

6.1 事件的三段结构6.1 The Three Phases of an Event

每一次已知日期的事件——财报、FDA 审批、议息、公投——期权价格都会走出同一个形状，三段分明： Every event with a known date — earnings, an FDA decision, a rate meeting, a referendum — drives option prices through the same three-phase shape:

① 堆积① Build-up
IV 缓慢上升，事件溢价累积IV grinds up, premium accrues
② 落地② Resolution
结果公布，股价跳空Result lands, the stock gaps
③ 蒸发③ Evaporation
IV 在数分钟内塌回常态IV collapses to baseline in minutes

TYPICAL EARNINGS WEEK · IV （典型形态示意）(illustrative shape)

第三段是关键，也是最被低估的一段。IV Crush 不是"有时会发生的意外"，它是结构性的、几乎必然的：事件溢价存在的唯一理由是不确定性，结果一公布，不确定性就消失了，溢价没有任何理由继续存在。你可以把它当成一件已知会在某个时刻发生的事来规划——而绝大多数亏钱的人，是把它当成运气不好。 The third phase is decisive and the most underrated. IV crush isn't an occasional accident; it is structural and very nearly certain: event premium exists only because of uncertainty, and the moment results are published the uncertainty is gone, leaving the premium no reason to exist. You can plan around it as a scheduled event — and most people who lose money treat it as bad luck.

关于本章的所有期权价格About every option price in this chapter

股价、日期和事件均为真实历史数据。期权价格由 Black-Scholes 计算，输入的 IV 已按当时市场公开引用的预期波动幅度反向校准（例如 META 校准到约 144% 的事件 IV，以复现市场当时报出的 ±8.5%）。它们是模型值而非成交回报单；真实链上的偏斜会让具体数字有出入，但量级和结论成立。 Share prices, dates and events are real history. Option prices are Black-Scholes values, with the IV input reverse-calibrated to the expected move the market publicly quoted at the time (META, for instance, calibrates to about 144% event IV to reproduce the ±8.5% then being quoted). They are model figures, not fills; skew on a real chain shifts the exact numbers, but the magnitudes and conclusions hold.

6.2 IV Crush 的解剖6.2 Anatomy of an IV Crush

第 3 章给过 IV Crush 一个精确的定义：Vega 乘以一个很大的负数。现在把它算出来。一张财报前的期权，其价格中包含着一块"事件溢价"——它等于 Vega × (事件 IV − 常态 IV)。财报后，这一块整块消失，与股价往哪走无关。 Chapter 3 gave IV crush an exact definition: vega times a large negative number. Now compute it. A pre-earnings option's price contains a block of "event premium" equal to vega × (event IV − baseline IV). After the report that block vanishes entirely, regardless of which way the stock went.

这产生了买方必须跨过的一道门槛：股价的实际移动，必须大到足以补偿这块必然蒸发的溢价。而市场定价的预期波动幅度，恰恰就是这道门槛的高度。第 5 章的 ATM Straddle 估法在这里再次派上用场：股价 $200，财报前跨式 $16，那么 ±8% 就是你的及格线——不是"涨就赚"，是"涨超过 8% 才赚"。 This creates a threshold buyers must clear: the stock's actual move must be large enough to compensate for premium that is guaranteed to evaporate. And the market's expected move is exactly the height of that threshold. Chapter 5's ATM-straddle estimate applies again: stock at $200 with a $16 pre-earnings straddle makes ±8% your passing grade — not "up means profit," but "up more than 8% means profit."

于是事件交易变成了一道干净的判断题，只有三个可能的答案： Event trading therefore reduces to a clean question with only three possible answers:

我认为会超出预期幅度More than priced
做买方Be the buyer
我认为会小于预期幅度Less than priced
做卖方（定义风险）Be the seller (defined risk)
我不知道I don't know
不交易No trade

请注意第三个答案和前两个是平等的。它不是懦弱，它是这道判断题的一个合法解——事实上是最常见的正解。绝大多数财报，你并不比市场知道得更多。 Note the third answer is the equal of the first two. It isn't timidity, it's a legitimate solution to the question — in fact the most common correct one. For the vast majority of earnings, you do not know more than the market.

6.3 当尾部真的超出定价6.3 When the Tail Really Does Exceed the Price

CASE FILE · 2022-02

Meta 的一夜：-26.4%，是定价的三倍Meta's One Night: −26.4%, Three Times the Price

2022 年 2 月 2 日盘后，Meta 公布 2021 年四季报。财报本身并不惨烈，但两件事击穿了市场：Facebook 日活用户出现史上第一次环比下降，以及远低于预期的下季度指引。2 月 2 日收盘 $323.00，2 月 3 日收盘 $237.76——单日 -26.4%，蒸发约 2,320 亿美元市值，当时美股史上最大的单日市值损失。 After the close on February 2, 2022, Meta reported Q4 2021. The quarter itself wasn't a disaster, but two things broke the market: Facebook's daily active users declined sequentially for the first time ever, alongside guidance far below expectations. February 2 closed at $323.00; February 3 closed at $237.76 — −26.4% in a session, erasing roughly $232 billion of market value, then the largest single-day loss in US market history.

META · FEB 2022 （收盘价）(closes)

财报前，期权市场报出的预期波动幅度约为 ±8.5%。实际走了 -26.4%——约为定价的 3.1 倍。这是第 5 章 Brexit 案例的完美反面：那一次史上最大的单日跌幅只是刚好够到定价，这一次实际结果把定价远远甩在身后。 Going in, the option market quoted an expected move of roughly ±8.5%. The realized move was −26.4% — about 3.1× the price. This is the perfect inverse of Chapter 5's Brexit case: there, a record one-day fall only just reached the price; here, reality left the price far behind.

三种表达同一个看空判断的方式，结果如下（按上述模型口径）： Three ways of expressing the same bearish view produced the following (on the model basis stated above):

做空正股Short the stock
+26.4%
买 $300 PutLong $300 put
$4.79 → $62.24 = +1,199%
买 300/270 Put 价差300/270 put spread
$4.19 → $30.00 = +616%

这是第 4 章那个"封顶的代价"第一次真正咬人的场景。价差的成本只比裸买 Put 低 12%，但它把收益锁死在 $30——而实际的内在价值走到了 $62。当尾部真的发生时，价差交出了一半的收益。第 4 章说"只有在你没预料到的爆发式行情里才明显吃亏"，这一次就是那个"没预料到"。 This is the first time Chapter 4's "cost of the cap" genuinely bites. The spread cost only 12% less than the outright put, but sealed the payoff at $30 while intrinsic value ran to $62. When the tail actually arrives, the spread hands back half the return. Chapter 4 said spreads only clearly lose "in the explosive move you didn't foresee" — this was that move.

利润扩大：同一个看空判断，做空正股 +26.4% → 买入 $300 Put +1,199%Profit amplified: the same bearish view, +26.4% short the stock → +1,199% via the $300 put

别把结果当成方法Don't mistake the outcome for the method

+1,199% 这个数字有毒。它诱使人得出"财报前买价外期权"这个结论，而这恰恰是最赔钱的习惯之一。请记住这张 $300 Put 的另一面：如果 Meta 那晚只跌了 8%（正是市场定价的幅度），它到期时值 $0，归零。能让它值 $62 的，是一个约为定价三倍的尾部事件——按定义，这种事很少发生。买入事件期权的正确心态不是"这次会大动"，而是"我认为市场对幅度的定价系统性偏低，并且我愿意为这个判断反复付出小额归零"。第 5 章的 Brexit 案例就是同一枚硬币的另一面：那一次，尾部发生了，买方依然没赚到钱。 That +1,199% is toxic. It tempts people into "buy OTM options before earnings," one of the most reliably expensive habits there is. Remember the other face of that $300 put: had Meta fallen only 8% — exactly what was priced — it would have expired at $0. What made it worth $62 was a tail roughly three times the price, and by definition that is rare. The correct mentality for buying event options isn't "this one will move big," it's "I believe the market systematically underprices magnitude here, and I accept repeatedly paying small amounts that go to zero." Chapter 5's Brexit case is the same coin's other side: there the tail did arrive, and buyers still made nothing.

6.4 好消息也能亏钱6.4 Good News Can Still Lose You Money

CASE FILE · 2021-01

苹果史上最好的一个季度，和它次日的 -3.5%Apple's Best Quarter Ever, and the −3.5% That Followed

2021 年 1 月 27 日盘后，苹果公布了公司历史上最好的一份财报：单季营收 1,114 亿美元，史上第一次单季突破千亿，同比增长约 21%，每股收益超出预期。任何一个只看基本面的人都会说这是碾压式的胜利。 After the close on January 27, 2021, Apple reported the best quarter in its history: $111.4 billion of revenue, the first quarter ever above $100 billion, up roughly 21% year over year, with EPS above expectations. Anyone reading only the fundamentals would call it a rout.

次日，1 月 28 日，苹果收在 $137.09，较前一日的 $142.06 下跌 -3.5%。 The next day, January 28, Apple closed at $137.09, down −3.5% from the prior day's $142.06.

AAPL · JAN 2021 （收盘价）(closes)

为什么？因为市场早就知道了。苹果在财报前两个月已经从约 $122 涨到约 $143，涨幅本身就是市场在为这份好财报定价；而公司当时并未提供下季度指引。财报前的预期波动幅度约 ±5.6%，实际只走了 -3.5%——波动落在了定价区间之内。对期权买方来说，这是最坏的组合：方向不利，幅度不足，IV 还塌了。 Because the market already knew. Apple had run from about $122 to about $143 in the two months before the report — that run was the market pricing the good quarter — and the company gave no forward guidance. The pre-earnings expected move was about ±5.6%, and the realized move was just −3.5%: inside the priced range. For an option buyer that is the worst possible combination: wrong direction, insufficient magnitude, and a collapsing IV on top.

三个同样"看多苹果"的人，一夜之后的处境： Three people, all bullish on Apple, the morning after:

直接持股Held the stock
−3.5%
卖 138/130 Put 价差Sold 138/130 put spread
约 −12%（按风险计）≈ −12% on risk
裸买 $142 CallBought the $142 call
$4.04 → $0.76 = −81%

同一个正确的公司判断，三种结构，亏损相差 23 倍。那张 Call 的亏损里，约 40% 直接来自 IV 从 45% 塌到 30%——即使股价原地不动，它也会亏掉三分之一。这就是"方向对了还亏钱"的完整机制：你买的从来不只是方向，你同时买了一份在结果公布那一刻必然作废的不确定性。 One correct read on the company, three structures, a 23-fold difference in loss. About 40% of that call's loss came directly from IV falling 45% to 30% — with the stock frozen it would still have lost a third. This is the full mechanism behind "right on direction, still down money": you never bought direction alone, you also bought a piece of uncertainty that was guaranteed to be voided the instant results were published.

亏损减少：同一个看多苹果的判断，裸买财报前 Call −81% → 直接持股 −3.5%Loss reduced: the same bullish view, −81% buying the pre-earnings call → −3.5% simply holding shares

思考Think 苹果那份财报无可挑剔，股价却跌了。假设你在财报前准确预知了营收会突破千亿——这个信息能让你赚钱吗？如果答案是"不能"，那么财报交易里真正有价值的信息到底是什么形状的？提示：它一定不是"公司会好还是坏"这种形状。 Apple's quarter was flawless and the stock fell. Suppose you had known with certainty, in advance, that revenue would break $100 billion — could you have made money on it? If the answer is no, then what shape does genuinely valuable information take in earnings trading? Hint: it is definitely not the shape of "will the company do well or badly."

6.5 结构选择：买、卖，还是不交易6.5 Structure Choice: Buy, Sell, or Stand Aside

把前面两个案例并排放，事件交易的结构选择就清楚了。它取决于一件事：你对幅度的判断，与市场定价的差在哪一边。 Set the two cases side by side and the structural choice becomes clear. It turns on one thing: which side of the market's price your own view of magnitude falls on.

认为会超出定价（买方）。买裸期权而不是价差——META 的案例说明，当尾部真的发生时，价差会交出一半收益。但请把仓位控制在"归零也不影响你"的水平，因为这类判断的胜率天然很低。认为会小于定价（卖方）。务必用定义风险的结构——Iron Condor 或贷记价差，永远不要裸卖。财报的尾部就是 META 那样的 3.1 倍，而裸卖方在那种夜晚没有底。 You think it exceeds the price (buy). Buy the outright option rather than the spread — the Meta case shows the spread surrenders half the return when the tail truly arrives. But size it so that a total loss doesn't matter, because this kind of call has an inherently low win rate. You think it falls short of the price (sell). Use a defined-risk structure without exception — an iron condor or a credit spread, never a naked short. The tail on earnings looks like Meta's 3.1×, and a naked seller has no floor on that kind of night.

AAPL 138/130 BULL PUT SPREAD · 卖方视角的同一个看多观点· the same bullish view, from the seller's side

还有第三条路，常被忽略：Calendar Spread（日历价差）。卖出财报当周的合约、买入下一周或下个月的同 Strike 合约。它的逻辑很精巧：近月的事件溢价最高、塌得最狠，远月塌得轻，所以你卖掉塌得最狠的那个。这是专门为"我确信 IV 会塌，但不想赌方向"这个观点设计的结构。代价是它需要股价停在 Strike 附近——一次 META 式的跳空同样会伤到它。 There's a third path people overlook: the calendar spread. Sell the contract expiring in earnings week and buy the same strike a week or a month out. The logic is neat: the near-dated contract holds the most event premium and crushes hardest, the far-dated one crushes less, so you sell the one that collapses most. It is purpose-built for "I'm confident IV collapses but I don't want a directional bet." Its cost is needing the stock to stay near the strike — a Meta-style gap damages it too.

与实盘的连接Bridge to live trading

① 财报日期要自己核对。公司会改期，而一次改期能让你的"事件期权"变成一张纯粹的时间损耗票。② 盘后不要试图平仓。财报后的盘后时段，期权买卖价差可以宽到毫无意义，你会在极差的价格上成交；有计划的做法是持有到次日开盘后流动性恢复。③ 用第 5 章那个除法。下单前算一次 ATM Straddle 总价 ÷ 股价，把这个百分比写下来。如果你说不出自己的判断在这个数字的哪一边，你就没有交易理由——这时"不交易"不是放弃，是这道题的正确答案。 ① Verify the earnings date yourself. Companies reschedule, and one reschedule turns your "event option" into a pure time-decay ticket. ② Don't try to close after hours. In the post-earnings session option spreads can widen to the point of meaninglessness and you will get a terrible fill; the planned approach is to hold until liquidity returns after the next open. ③ Do Chapter 5's division. Before entering, compute the ATM straddle total ÷ share price and write that percentage down. If you can't say which side of that number your view falls on, you have no reason to trade — and "no trade" isn't giving up, it's this question's correct answer.

6.6 本章测验6.6 Chapter Quiz

苹果公布了史上最好的季报，股价次日却跌 3.5%。最合理的解释是？Apple posts its best-ever quarter and the stock falls 3.5% the next day. The most reasonable explanation:

C。苹果在财报前一个月已从约 $122 涨到约 $143——那段涨幅本身就是市场在给这份好财报定价。股价反应的从来不是"消息好不好"，而是"消息比已经被计入的预期好多少"。A 是无法证伪的托词；B 与事实不符（该季各项指标均超预期）；D 是阴谋论。 C. Apple had already run from about $122 to about $143 in the preceding month — that run was the market pricing the good quarter. Prices never react to "is the news good," they react to "how much better is the news than what was already priced." A is an unfalsifiable excuse; B is false (every line beat); D is conspiracy.

财报前 ATM Straddle 报价 $12，股价 $150。财报后股价上涨 5%。买入 ATM Call 的人大概率？A pre-earnings ATM straddle is $12 with the stock at $150. After earnings the stock rises 5%. The ATM call buyer most likely:

B。$12 ÷ $150 = 8%，这是市场定价的预期波动幅度，也是买方的及格线。实际走了 5%，落在定价区间之内——涨幅带来的内在价值不足以补偿必然蒸发的事件溢价。这正是苹果 2021 年 1 月那个案例的机制。A 是本章要根治的直觉；D 不成立，题目已经给全了两个必要数字。 B. $12 ÷ $150 = 8%, the market's expected move and the buyer's passing grade. The realized 5% fell inside the priced range — the intrinsic value gained doesn't cover the event premium that was always going to evaporate. This is precisely the mechanism of Apple's January 2021 case. A is the intuition this chapter exists to cure; D fails because both necessary numbers are given.

Meta 那晚跌了定价的 3.1 倍。为什么裸买 Put（+1,199%）远胜 Put 价差（+616%）？Meta fell 3.1× the priced move. Why did the outright put (+1,199%) so far outrun the put spread (+616%)?

A。300/270 价差的最大收益就是宽度 $30；股价跌到 $237.76 时，裸 Put 的内在价值已是 $62.24。多出来的 $32 全部被下腿吃掉了。B 说反了（价差 $4.19 比裸买 $4.79 便宜）；C 也说反了——价差的净 Vega 更小，受 IV Crush 影响更轻，这正是它平时的优点；D 与事实不符。这道题的价值在于：第 4 章说的"封顶代价"不是理论，它有一个具体的名字叫 $32。 A. The 300/270 spread's maximum is its $30 width; at $237.76 the outright put held $62.24 of intrinsic value. The extra $32 was consumed entirely by the lower leg. B is backwards (the spread cost $4.19 against $4.79). C is backwards too — a spread's smaller net vega makes it less exposed to IV crush, which is normally its advantage. D is false. The value of this question: Chapter 4's "cost of the cap" isn't theory — here it has a specific name, and that name is $32.

你判断某公司财报后的波动会小于市场定价。最合适的做法是？You believe a company will move less than the market has priced. The most appropriate action:

D。观点本身（波动小于定价）指向卖方，这排除了 B、C。但 A 是这一章最危险的选项：财报的尾部就是 Meta 那样的 3.1 倍，裸卖 Straddle 在那种夜晚没有底——一次就足以结束账户。定义风险的结构把最坏结果在开仓时就固定下来，这是在事件面前做卖方的唯一负责任的方式。 D. The view itself (less movement than priced) points to selling, ruling out B and C. But A is this chapter's most dangerous option: the tail on earnings looks like Meta's 3.1×, and a naked straddle has no floor on that night — one is enough to end an account. A defined-risk structure fixes the worst case at entry, which is the only responsible way to be a seller into an event.

财报后你想立刻在盘后平掉获利的 Put。为什么这通常是个坏主意？You want to close a winning put in the after-hours session right after earnings. Why is that usually a bad idea?

C。美股期权本身在常规时段之外基本没有流动性，正股盘后波动剧烈时做市商会把双边报价拉得极宽——你可能在远低于理论价值的位置成交，把辛苦赚到的尾部收益还回去一大块。A 措辞过于绝对但方向接近：真正的问题不是"不能"，而是"能，但价格极差"。有计划的做法是等次日开盘流动性恢复。 C. US equity options have essentially no liquidity outside regular hours, and when the underlying is swinging after the bell market makers widen quotes dramatically — you can fill far below theoretical value and give back a large slice of the tail gain you just earned. A overstates it but points the right way: the problem isn't that you can't, it's that you can at a terrible price. The planned approach is to wait for liquidity at the next open.

本章要点Key Takeaways

事件交易只有一个真问题：实际波动会大于还是小于市场已经定好的价格，而那个价格就明明白白写在 ATM Straddle 的报价里。Meta 在 2022 年 2 月走出了定价 3.1 倍的跌幅，让一张 $300 的 Put 变成 12 倍；苹果在 2021 年 1 月交出史上最好的财报，却让一张 ATM Call 亏掉 81%——两件事都不是运气，它们是同一道除法题的两个答案。IV Crush 不是意外而是日程表上的事项：事件溢价存在的唯一理由是不确定性，结果公布，理由消失。所以站在买方要接受高频率的归零，站在卖方必须用定义风险的结构——因为财报的尾部就长着 Meta 那副样子。而当你算完那个百分比却说不出自己站在它的哪一边时，"不交易"就是这道题的正确答案，和另外两个答案完全平等。 Event trading poses exactly one real question: will the realized move be larger or smaller than the price already set — and that price is written plainly in the ATM straddle's quote. Meta fell 3.1× its priced move in February 2022, turning a $300 put into a twelve-bagger; Apple delivered the best quarter in its history in January 2021 and an ATM call still lost 81%. Neither was luck; they are two answers to the same division problem. IV crush isn't an accident but a calendar item: event premium exists only because of uncertainty, and when results publish the reason disappears. So buyers must accept frequent total losses, and sellers must use defined-risk structures — because the tail on earnings is shaped exactly like Meta's. And when you've done the division and still can't say which side you're on, "no trade" is this question's correct answer, fully equal to the other two.
