Chapter 02 · Single-Leg Strategies

单腿策略：一份合约能做什么Single-Leg Strategies: What One Contract Can Do

所有复杂的期权组合，拆开都是四种基本头寸的排列。这一章讲清楚其中最实用的四种用法：买保险、买杠杆、收租金、挂低价买单。 Every complex options structure decomposes into four primitive positions. This chapter covers the four most practical uses: buying insurance, buying leverage, collecting rent, and placing a limit order that pays you to wait.

Long Call 的本质是把"看涨"这个观点浓缩成一笔有限的支出。你花一笔 Premium，换取在 Expiration（到期日）之前以 Strike（行权价）买入股票的权利。涨得多，收益按倍数放大；判断错了，最多亏掉 Premium——亏损上限在下单那一刻就锁死了。 A long call compresses a bullish view into a fixed, known cost. You pay a premium for the right to buy the stock at the strike any time before expiration. If the stock runs, your gain multiplies; if you're wrong, you lose at most the premium — the downside is capped the moment you enter.

适用场景：① 你对某个催化剂（财报、发布会、政策）有强方向判断，且愿意为"判断错就归零"付费；② 你想参与上涨但不想占用买 100 股的资金；③ 替代追高——用 Call 锁定买入价，涨了行权，跌了只损失 Premium。 When to use it: ① You have a strong directional view on a catalyst (earnings, product launch, policy) and accept that a wrong call means the premium goes to zero; ② you want upside exposure without committing capital for 100 shares; ③ as an alternative to chasing a breakout — the call locks in your entry price, and a failed breakout costs only the premium.

买方的两个隐形税The two hidden taxes on option buyers

① Theta：每天醒来，你的期权都比昨天便宜一点，临近到期加速。② IV Crush：财报等事件落地后，Implied Volatility 骤降，哪怕股价小涨，期权价格也可能不涨反跌。买方赚钱需要"方向对 + 幅度够 + 时间内"三者同时成立。 ① Theta: your option is worth a little less every morning, and the decay accelerates near expiry. ② IV crush: after an event like earnings, implied volatility collapses — the stock can drift up while your call loses value. Buyers need direction, magnitude, and timing to all be right.

CASE FILE · 2023-05

NVIDIA 财报夜：一次教科书级的 Long CallNVIDIA's Earnings Night: A Textbook Long Call

2023 年 5 月 24 日盘后，NVIDIA 公布财报并给出远超预期的 AI 需求指引，次日股价从约 $305 跳空到约 $379，单日 +24%——大市值股票史上罕见的跳空。财报前买入下周到期、Strike $320 的 OTM Call 大约只需 $4 出头；财报后该合约内在价值已接近 $60，回报约 13 倍，而同期正股持有者赚 24%。 After the close on May 24, 2023, NVIDIA reported earnings with AI-demand guidance far above expectations. The next day the stock gapped from roughly $305 to $379 — +24% in a session, a historic gap for a mega-cap. Before earnings, an OTM call at the $320 strike expiring that week cost around $4; after the report it carried nearly $60 of intrinsic value — roughly a 13× return versus +24% for shareholders.

这就是 Long Call 的"扩大利润"一面：方向、幅度、时间三者全对时，收益是非线性的。但反面同样成立——如果那晚指引平淡，股价原地踏步，这张 $4 的 Call 会因 IV Crush 和临近到期在两天内归零。财报博弈是硬币的两面，第 6 章会讲如何用价差结构把这枚硬币变得不那么极端。 This is the profit-amplifying face of the long call: when direction, magnitude, and timing all align, the payoff is nonlinear. The reverse is equally true — with in-line guidance and a flat stock, that $4 call would have gone to zero within two days from IV crush and expiry. Earnings are a two-sided coin; Chapter 6 shows how spreads make that coin less extreme.

Protective Put（保护性买入）Protective put

Long Put 有两种人格。投机人格：看跌某只股票，用有限成本做空。保险人格更重要：持有股票的同时买入 Put（即 Protective Put），相当于给持仓上了一份"跌破 Strike 全额理赔"的保单——Premium 就是保费，Strike 就是免赔线。 The long put has two personalities. The speculative one: a limited-cost way to short a stock. The insurance one matters more: holding shares and buying a put against them (a protective put) is a policy that pays out in full below the strike — the premium is your insurance cost, the strike is your deductible line.

适用场景：① 长期仓位不想卖（税务、基本面信仰），但担心短期系统性风险；② 浮盈很大，想锁住大部分利润又保留上行空间；③ 事件前的定向对冲（财报、加息决议）。保险不是常态配置——长期持续买 Put 的成本会显著侵蚀收益，它是特定时期的战术工具。 When to use it: ① You hold a long-term position you don't want to sell (taxes, conviction) but fear near-term systemic risk; ② you're sitting on large gains and want to lock in most of them while keeping upside; ③ targeted hedging ahead of an event (earnings, a rate decision). Insurance is not a permanent allocation — buying puts continuously is a serious drag on returns. It's a tactical tool for specific windows.

CASE FILE · 2020-02

COVID 崩盘：一张 Put 的价值The COVID Crash: What One Put Was Worth

2020 年 2 月 19 日，SPY 创下约 $337 的历史新高。此后五周，疫情引发史上最快的熊市，3 月 23 日 SPY 最低触及约 $218，高点回撤超过 -34%。 On February 19, 2020, SPY printed an all-time high near $337. Over the next five weeks the pandemic triggered the fastest bear market in history; on March 23 SPY touched roughly $218 — a drawdown of more than -34% from the peak.

Counterfactual：假设你持有 100 股 SPY，在 2 月 21 日（市场刚开始松动、恐慌尚未定价时）花约 $5/股买入 3 月底到期、Strike $330 的 Protective Put。此后无论 SPY 跌到哪里，你的组合价值被锁定在约 $325/股（$330 理赔线 − $5 保费）。裸持仓最深亏 -34%，戴保险的持仓最深亏约 -4%。 Counterfactual: suppose you held 100 SPY shares and on February 21 — when the market had just begun to wobble but panic wasn't yet priced in — paid about $5/share for a protective put, strike $330, expiring end of March. From then on, no matter how far SPY fell, your portfolio was floored at roughly $325/share ($330 payout line minus the $5 premium). The unhedged position drew down -34%; the insured one, about -4%.

SPY · FEB–MAR 2020 （周收盘，约值，每股口径）(weekly closes, approx., per share)

注意时点的重要性：2 月 21 日 IV 还未飙升，这张保单是"便宜"的；等到 3 月中旬 VIX 超过 80 时，同样的保护要贵出数倍——保险要在着火前买。这也是为什么 Protective Put 是战术工具：它的性价比取决于你是否在市场自满时买入。 Timing was everything: on February 21, IV had not yet exploded and this policy was cheap. By mid-March, with the VIX above 80, the same protection cost several times more — you buy insurance before the fire. This is why the protective put is a tactical tool: its value-for-money depends on buying while the market is still complacent.

亏损减少：-34% 回撤 → 约 -4%，代价是约 1.5% 的保费Loss reduced: a -34% drawdown → about -4%, for a premium cost of ≈1.5%

If you bought this insurance every month for a year, roughly what would it cost annually? How does that number change your answer to "should I stay hedged all the time"?

持有 100 股，同时卖出一张 Call——你把"股价涨过 Strike 之后的收益"卖给了别人，换来一笔确定的 Premium。它不改变你的下跌风险（股票该跌还是跌，只是有 Premium 垫着），改变的是收益的形状：牺牲不确定的大涨，换取确定的现金流。 Hold 100 shares, sell one call against them — you've sold someone the upside beyond the strike in exchange for certain cash today. It doesn't change your downside (the stock falls just the same, cushioned only by the premium); it changes the shape of your return: trading uncertain large gains for certain income.

适用场景：① 判断标的将横盘或缓涨，愿意在某个价位减仓；② 长期持仓想增强现金流；③ 震荡下行的市场里给持仓"垫缓冲"。核心问题只有一个：Strike 就是你的预设卖出价——如果你不愿意在 Strike 卖掉股票，就不要在那个价位卖 Call。 When to use it: ① You expect the stock to chop sideways or grind up, and you'd be content to trim at some price; ② you want cash flow from a long-term holding; ③ you want a cushion in a choppy, declining market. There is really only one question: the strike is your pre-committed sale price — if you wouldn't be happy selling shares at the strike, don't sell the call there.

把同样的策略搬到 2023 年的 NVIDIA 上就是灾难性的机会成本：正股全年 +239%，而每月卖 5% OTM Call 的组合会在 5 月财报跳空那一刻被按 Strike 收走股票，错过其后的绝大部分涨幅。Covered Call 的隐性成本不是亏钱，而是在单边牛市里"赚小钱、丢大钱"。策略没有好坏，只有与市场状态（Regime）的匹配与错配。 Run the same playbook on NVIDIA in 2023 and it becomes a catastrophic opportunity cost: the stock returned +239% for the year, while a monthly 5%-OTM covered call would have had the shares called away at the strike the moment of the May earnings gap, missing most of what followed. The hidden cost of covered calls isn't losing money — it's earning small money while forfeiting big money in a one-way bull market. Strategies aren't good or bad; they're matched or mismatched to the regime.

If you find yourself angry after an assignment, what judgment did you skip at the moment you opened the trade?

卖出一张 Put，同时锁定足额现金准备接货。它是 Covered Call 的镜像：Covered Call 是"持股者的预设卖单"，CSP 是"想买者的预设买单"——而且这张买单是别人付钱请你挂的。股价跌破 Strike，你按 Strike 接货（实际成本 = Strike − Premium，比直接挂限价单便宜）；没跌破，Premium 落袋，下月再来。 Sell one put and set aside the full cash to buy the shares if assigned. It's the mirror of the covered call: a covered call is a shareholder's pre-committed sell order; a CSP is a buyer's pre-committed buy order — one that someone pays you to place. If the stock falls through the strike, you buy at the strike (effective cost = strike − premium, cheaper than a plain limit order); if it doesn't, you keep the premium and do it again next month.

适用场景：① 想买某只股票，但觉得现价偏贵，愿意在更低的价格接；② 系统化收租（CSP → 被指派 → 转 Covered Call，就是第 7 章的 Wheel）。前提永远只有一个：Strike 是你真心愿意持有这家公司的价格，Premium 只是耐心的利息，不是策略的目的。为了肥美的 Premium 去卖你根本不想接的股票的 Put，是这个策略最常见的死法。 When to use it: ① You want a stock but find today's price rich, and would happily buy lower; ② systematic income (CSP → assignment → covered call is the Wheel, Chapter 7). There is exactly one precondition: the strike must be a price at which you genuinely want to own the business. The premium is interest on your patience, not the point. Selling puts on stocks you'd hate to own, because the premium looks juicy, is this strategy's most common cause of death.

INTERACTIVE · CASH-SECURED PUT

CASE FILE · 1993-04

巴菲特的可口可乐 Put：机构级 CSPBuffett's Coca-Cola Puts: A CSP at Institutional Scale

1993 年 4 月，巴菲特想继续增持可口可乐，但认为当时约 $39 的股价不够便宜。他没有挂限价单干等，而是卖出了 5 万张（对应 500 万股）当年 12 月到期、Strike $35 的 Put，每股收取 $1.50——一次性入账约 750 万美元。 In April 1993, Buffett wanted to add to Berkshire's Coca-Cola stake but considered the ~$39 price not cheap enough. Instead of parking a limit order, he sold 50,000 put contracts (covering 5 million shares), December expiry, strike $35, collecting $1.50 per share — roughly $7.5 million of premium up front.

两种结局对他都是赢：股价跌破 $35，他以实际成本 $33.50 买入他本来就想要的公司；股价不跌（实际结局），$750 万权利金无成本落袋。这笔交易把 CSP 的本质说得再清楚不过：它不是做空波动率的投机，而是把"我愿意在什么价格买入"这个决策货币化。 Both outcomes were wins by construction: below $35, he'd buy a business he already wanted at an effective $33.50; above it (what actually happened), $7.5M of premium was banked at no cost. The trade states the essence of the CSP perfectly: it's not a volatility short — it's the monetization of a decision you've already made about the price you'd pay.

Chapter 7's Wheel system covers entry discipline in depth: regime filters, "no trade" as a valid output, and true cost-basis accounting after assignment.

本章要点Key Takeaways

买方（Long Call / Long Put）用有限的 Premium 换取非线性的收益，但同时被 Theta 和 IV Crush 双重征税，需要方向、幅度、时间三者全对。有抵押的卖方（Covered Call / CSP）把时间变成朋友，用确定的现金流换取尾部收益或承接义务——它们本质上都是把你"愿意在什么价格买卖"的决策货币化。策略本身没有优劣：2020 年属于 Put 买方，2022 年属于 Call 卖方，2023 年属于 Call 买方。判断当下的市场状态，永远先于选择策略。 Buyers (long calls and puts) exchange a bounded premium for nonlinear payoffs, taxed all the while by theta and IV crush — direction, magnitude, and timing must all be right. Collateralized sellers (covered calls and CSPs) make time an ally, exchanging tail upside or an obligation to buy for certain cash flow — at heart, both monetize a decision you've already made about the prices at which you'd buy or sell. No strategy is inherently superior: 2020 belonged to put buyers, 2022 to call sellers, 2023 to call buyers. Reading the regime always precedes choosing the strategy.
