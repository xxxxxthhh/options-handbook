Chapter 15 · Evidence Literacy

证据素养：这一切长期到底值不值得做Evidence Literacy: Was Any of This Worth Doing

前十四章教的是怎么把观点做成结构。这一章问一个所有人都该在开始之前问、而几乎所有人都在亏钱之后才问的问题：这些策略在几十年的真实数据里，到底跑出了什么结果？本章是全书唯一一章没有任何示意值——所有数字都来自公开发布的基准指数，可以逐个核对。 The first fourteen chapters taught how to turn a view into a structure. This one asks the question everybody should ask before starting and almost everybody asks only after losing money: across decades of real data, what did these strategies actually produce? It is the only chapter in this book with no illustrative values at all — every figure comes from a published benchmark index and can be checked line by line.

15.1 为什么这一章放在最后15.1 Why This Chapter Comes Last

本书用了十四章讲结构，而每一个案例都是单次事件：NVDA 那一夜、COVID 那五周、CPI 那一天。单次事件能说明机制，但它说明不了期望。第 8 章已经点破过这个取样偏差——那些案例都是幸存下来的故事。要回答"长期值不值得做"，你需要的不是又一个精彩案例，而是一条不加挑选、连续几十年、包含所有好年份和所有坏年份的记录。 Fourteen chapters of structures, and every case was a single episode: that one NVIDIA night, those five COVID weeks, that one CPI day. Episodes demonstrate mechanism; they cannot establish expectancy. Chapter 8 named the sampling bias — those cases are all stories that survived. To answer "is this worth doing over a lifetime," you need not another vivid case but an unselected, continuous, decades-long record containing every good year and every bad one.

这样的记录是存在的。Cboe 从 1986 年起就在计算策略基准指数：BXM 机械地执行第 2 章的 Covered Call——持有标普 500，每月卖出一张平值 Call，月月如此，不择时、不挑标的、不跳过任何一个月。它是"如果一个人完美执行这个策略四十年会怎样"的答案，而且这个答案是公开的。 Such a record exists. Cboe has computed strategy benchmark indices since 1986: BXM mechanically runs Chapter 2's covered call — hold the S&P 500, sell one at-the-money call every month, every month, with no timing, no selection, and no skipped months. It is the answer to "what if someone executed this perfectly for forty years," and the answer is public.

两份数据都出自可信来源，起点几乎相同，结论却相差 4.25 个百分点——一边是期权卖方明显跑赢，一边是明显跑输。最大的差别不在方法，在那个截止日期。2008 年 10 月是什么时候？标普 500 已经从高点腰斩、离最终低点只剩几个月。把一个 Beta 只有 0.62 的策略，测量到七十年来最惨崩盘的谷底为止，它当然会赢——这个结论几乎是被截止日期决定的，而不是被策略决定的。 Both figures come from credible sources over near-identical start dates, and their conclusions differ by 4.25 percentage points — one showing option writers clearly ahead, the other clearly behind. The largest difference isn't methodology; it's that end date. What was October 2008? The S&P had already halved from its peak and was months from its final low. Measure a strategy with a beta of 0.62 up to the bottom of the worst crash in seventy years and of course it wins — that conclusion is very nearly determined by the end date rather than by the strategy.

在你比较任何两个收益率之前Before you compare any two return figures

上面两组数字严格来说甚至不能直接相减，而这本身就是本章要教的东西。它们至少有三处不可比：① 指数不同——PUT 卖 Put、BXM 卖 Call，是两个不同的策略指数；② 窗口不同——差了近十八年，且一个终止于崩盘谷底；③ 对照口径可能不同——标普 500 的"价格收益"与"全收益"相差每年约两个百分点（股息），而很多材料不写清楚用的是哪一个。 Strictly, those two sets of numbers cannot even be subtracted from one another — and that is itself the lesson. They differ in at least three ways: ① different indices — PUT sells puts, BXM sells calls; two different strategies; ② different windows — nearly eighteen years apart, one ending at a crash bottom; ③ possibly different benchmark variants — the S&P 500's "price return" and "total return" differ by roughly two points a year (dividends), and a great deal of material never says which one it used.

所以这一节真正的产出不是"卖方到底赢没赢"，而是一个习惯：看到任何一个"某策略年化跑赢 X 个百分点"的说法，先问三个问题——哪个指数、哪个窗口、对照的是价格收益还是全收益。三个问题里只要有一个答不上来，那个数字就不能用来做决策。 So this section's output isn't "did writers win" but a habit: whenever you meet a claim that some strategy beat the market by X points a year, ask three questions first — which index, which window, and price return or total return. If even one has no answer, that number cannot be used to make a decision.

15.4 指数不是基金15.4 An Index Is Not a Fund

还有一层，在上面所有数字之外。BXM 是一个指数，不是一只你能买的基金。Cboe 自己的免责声明写得毫不含糊：指数值是理论值，不反映实际交易结果，也不反映买卖、持有相关投资所产生的费用、税负或其他开支。 There is one more layer beneath all of the above. BXM is an index, not a fund you can buy. Cboe's own disclaimer is unambiguous: index values are theoretical, do not reflect the results of actual trading, and do not reflect the fees, taxes or other expenses associated with buying, holding or selling investments.

把 8.5% 变成你账户里的数字，至少要再扣掉四层：① 管理费——追踪买写策略的 ETF 管理费率通常在 0.6%–0.8% 量级；② 每月的买卖价差——BXM 假设按结算价成交，真实世界每月要穿越一次点差；③ 税——每月卖出的 Call 产生持续的应税事件，而买入持有指数不产生（本书不提供税务建议，但这一项在应税账户里不可忽略）；④ 执行偏差——指数从不错过一个月、从不手滑、从不因为"这个月 IV 太低"而跳过。 Turning 8.5% into a number in your account means subtracting at least four more layers: ① management fees — ETFs tracking buy-write strategies typically charge on the order of 0.6–0.8%; ② the monthly bid-ask — BXM assumes settlement-price fills, while the real world crosses a spread every month; ③ taxes — a call sold every month generates a continuous stream of taxable events that buying and holding an index does not (this book gives no tax advice, but in a taxable account the item is not ignorable); ④ execution drift — the index never misses a month, never fat-fingers, and never skips because "IV looks low this month."

这一层为什么是课程内容，而不是脚注Why this layer is the lesson, not a footnote

因为它不对称地作用于比较的两边。指数化持有标普 500 的成本可以低到每年 0.03%，而机械执行买写策略的成本是它的二十倍以上，还要加上每月的点差与税务摩擦。也就是说：8.5% 对 11.2% 这个已经不利的对比，在扣完真实成本之后只会更不利，而不会更好。凡是拿指数收益率去推销一个需要持续交易的策略的材料，都在系统性地高估你能拿到的部分——而且高估的方向永远是同一个。 Because it bites asymmetrically on the two sides of the comparison. Indexing the S&P 500 can cost as little as 0.03% a year, while running a mechanical buy-write costs twenty times that, plus a monthly spread and tax friction. Which means the already-unfavourable 8.5% versus 11.2% only gets worse after real costs, never better. Any material quoting index returns to sell a strategy that requires continuous trading is systematically overstating what you would receive — and the overstatement always points the same way.

没有。而弄清楚"为什么没错"，是本章最后、也最重要的一步。 No. And working out why not is this chapter's final and most important step.

同一个策略，同样是下跌年，一次少亏 6.7 个百分点，一次反而多亏 0.4 个百分点。这不是噪音，原因在第 2 章和第 9 章都写过：2022 年是持续一整年的阴跌——每个月都能顺利收租，租金月月累积，这正是 Covered Call 的理想环境；2018 年的下跌集中在第四季度的急挫，随后迅速反弹，而急涨会让卖出的 Call 被击穿、把反弹的收益削掉，于是"缓冲"没兑现，"封顶"却兑现了。 The same strategy, in two down years, was 6.7 points better in one and 0.4 points worse in the other. That isn't noise, and Chapters 2 and 9 already gave the reason: 2022 was a grinding decline lasting all year — every month's rent was collected cleanly and accumulated, which is a covered call's ideal habitat. 2018's decline was a sharp fourth-quarter drop followed by a fast rebound, and a fast rebound breaches the calls you sold and shaves off the recovery — so the cushion failed to materialise while the cap did.

所以四十年数据和第 2 章并不矛盾，它们在回答不同的问题。第 2 章问的是"在什么样的市场里这个结构有用"，答案是震荡与阴跌；BXM 问的是"不管什么市场都机械执行四十年会怎样"，答案是与指数打平、扣费后更差。两个答案合在一起才是完整的结论：这个策略本身是有效的，无效的是"不看 Regime 就一直做"。这正是本书从第 2 章讲到第 9 章的那条主线，只不过这一次它有了四十年的数据背书。 So the forty-year record and Chapter 2 don't contradict each other — they answer different questions. Chapter 2 asked "in what market is this structure useful," and the answer is chop and grinding declines. BXM asks "what if you ran it mechanically for forty years regardless," and the answer is a tie with the index, worse after costs. Together they form the complete conclusion: the strategy works; what doesn't work is running it without reading the regime. That is the through-line this book has followed since Chapter 2 — only now it has four decades of data behind it.

思考Think BXM 的四十年夏普比率是 0.55，标普是 0.56。如果一个策略在几十年里既没有创造超额收益、也没有改善风险调整后收益，那么它存在的意义是什么？在你回答"没有意义"之前，先想想第 9 章那条回撤曲线：−35.8% 和 −50.9% 对一个真实的人来说，是"继续持有"和"在谷底割肉"的区别。一个不改变期望值、但改变你能否坚持下去的工具，到底算不算有用？ BXM's forty-year Sharpe is 0.55; the S&P's is 0.56. If a strategy creates neither excess return nor better risk-adjusted return across four decades, what is it for? Before answering "nothing," revisit Chapter 9's drawdown curve: for an actual human being, −35.8% and −50.9% is the difference between holding on and capitulating at the bottom. Is a tool that doesn't change your expectancy but does change whether you can stay in the seat useful or not?

Crucially these costs are asymmetric: indexing the S&P can cost 0.03% a year while a buy-write's friction is many times that, so the already-unfavourable 8.5% versus 11.2% only worsens after costs.

2022 年 BXM 少亏 6.7 个百分点，2018 年却多亏 0.4 个百分点。为什么？BXM was 6.7 points better in 2022 but 0.4 points worse in 2018. Why?

A。这正是第 2 章与第 9 章那条主线在四十年数据里的显影：Covered Call 的缓冲来自"月月能顺利收租"，而不是来自"市场下跌"这件事本身。2022 年全年阴跌，每个月的租金都落袋；2018 年的跌幅集中在第四季度急挫、随后迅速反弹，反弹会击穿卖出的 Call，于是缓冲没兑现、封顶却兑现了。C 把一个条件性结论说成了必然，而这两年恰好证伪了它。决定它有没有用的是 Regime，不是"涨还是跌"。 A. This is the through-line of Chapters 2 and 9 developing in forty years of data: a covered call's cushion comes from collecting rent cleanly month after month, not from the market falling as such. 2022 ground lower all year and every month's rent was banked; 2018's decline was concentrated in a sharp Q4 drop followed by a rapid rebound, and rebounds breach the calls you sold — so the cushion never arrived while the cap did. C states a conditional result as a certainty, and these two years are precisely what falsifies it. What decides whether it helps is the regime, not merely up-or-down.

读到"某策略年化跑赢市场 X 个百分点"时，本章建议先问哪三个问题？On meeting a claim that "strategy X beats the market by N points a year," which three questions does this chapter say to ask first?

C。这三个问题决定了两个数字能不能被放在一起比较——本章那两组权威数据严格来说甚至不能相减：指数不同（PUT 卖 Put vs BXM 卖 Call）、窗口不同（差近十八年，一个终止于崩盘谷底）、对照口径可能不同（标普价格收益与全收益每年差约两个百分点）。B 和 D 都是好问题，但它们是第二步：在确认两个数字可比之前，算风险调整或扣费用都没有意义。A 关注来源可信度，而本章那两份数据都可信——问题不在可信度，在可比性。 C. Those three decide whether two numbers can be compared at all — the chapter's two authoritative datasets strictly cannot even be subtracted: different indices (PUT sells puts, BXM sells calls), different windows (nearly eighteen years apart, one ending at a crash bottom), possibly different benchmark variants (the S&P's price and total return differ by about two points a year). B and D are good questions but they are step two: risk-adjusting or netting out fees is meaningless before you've established the figures are comparable. A asks about credibility, yet both of this chapter's sources are credible — the problem isn't credibility, it's comparability.

本章要点Key Takeaways

十四章的案例能说明机制，说明不了期望；要回答"长期值不值得"，只能看一条不加挑选、连续几十年的记录。Cboe 的 BXM 就是这条记录：机械执行第 2 章的备兑开仓四十年，年化 8.5% 对标普全收益 11.2%，波动率 10.7% 对 15.2%，最大回撤 −35.8% 对 −50.9%。前面几个数字支持第 2 章的一切说法，而真正的答案藏在最后两行：夏普 0.55 对 0.56、索提诺 0.75 对 0.84——四十年之后，风险调整后的收益几乎完全相同，下行调整后还更差。它确实用收益换来了平稳，但这笔交换是公平的，没有免费午餐。更何况指数不含费用、点差与税，而这些成本不对称地作用于比较的两边，所以真实账户里的差距只会更大。 Fourteen chapters of cases can demonstrate mechanism but never expectancy; answering "is this worth doing" requires an unselected, decades-long record. Cboe's BXM is that record: Chapter 2's covered call run mechanically for forty years returned 8.5% annualized against the S&P's 11.2% total return, with 10.7% volatility against 15.2% and a worst drawdown of −35.8% against −50.9%. Those figures support everything Chapter 2 claimed — but the real answer sits in the last two rows: Sharpe 0.55 against 0.56, Sortino 0.75 against 0.84. After forty years the risk-adjusted returns are nearly identical, and on downside risk the strategy is worse. It did trade return for smoothness, and the trade was fair — there was no free lunch in it. And since the index excludes fees, spreads and taxes, which bite asymmetrically on the two sides, a real account's gap only widens.

但这并不推翻第 2 章，因为两者回答的是不同的问题。2022 年的阴跌里 BXM 少亏 6.7 个百分点，2018 年的 V 形下跌里却多亏 0.4 个百分点——同一个策略、同样是下跌年，结果相反。决定它有没有用的从来不是"涨还是跌"，而是 Regime。这正是本书从第 2 章讲到第 9 章的那条主线，现在它有了四十年的数据背书：策略本身是有效的，无效的是"不看状态就一直做"。至于那两份结论相反的权威数据——一份测到 2008 年 10 月的崩盘谷底，卖方跑赢 1.55 点；一份测到 2026 年 6 月，卖方跑输 2.7 点——它们留下的不是"谁对"，而是一个必须变成本能的习惯：看到任何一个跑赢多少个百分点的说法，先问哪个指数、哪个窗口、价格收益还是全收益。三个问题里有一个答不上来，那个数字就不能拿来做决策。这本书能教给你的最后一件事，不是任何一个结构，而是对数字本身保持怀疑的能力。 None of this overturns Chapter 2, because the two answer different questions. Through 2022's grinding decline BXM was 6.7 points better; through 2018's V-shaped fall it was 0.4 points worse — the same strategy, two down years, opposite results. What decides whether it helps was never up-or-down but the regime. That is the through-line running from Chapter 2 to Chapter 9, now with four decades behind it: the strategy works; running it without reading the regime does not. As for the two authoritative datasets that disagree — one measured to the crash bottom of October 2008, showing writers ahead by 1.55 points, one measured to June 2026, showing them behind by 2.7 — what they leave you is not "who was right" but a habit that has to become instinct: whenever you meet a claim of beating the market by N points, ask which index, which window, price return or total return. If even one has no answer, that number cannot inform a decision. The last thing this book can teach you is not a structure at all — it is the capacity to doubt a number.
